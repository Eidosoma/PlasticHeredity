# Discovery Log — Eidosoma AI Scientist

This date-only log follows the project from the original paper-replication attempt through the Eidosoma discovery program and the independent Codex and Fable branches. Each entry records the evidentiary status reached on that date; later entries may narrow the language of earlier findings without rewriting the historical result. Throughout this log, non-reproduction of the target paper refers only to our public-source clean-room reconstructions. At the relevant evidence cutoffs, target-specific code and a complete executable supplement had not yet been available, making implementation mismatch the leading explanation; the authors have since indicated that their implementation will be shared. These results neither test the authors' exact pipeline nor imply that their preprint is wrong. Candidate labels such as `02` and `03` are local to each implementation and do not imply identical simulator contracts across branches.

## Robert Bjarnason working with Eidosoma AI Scientist

### 2026-08-01

**Program launch.** E01 began as a structured public-source reconstruction of Pigozzi and Levin's *Causal Architecture Dynamics Prior to Arrival of Self-replicators in a Model of Catalytic Networks Relevant to Origin-of-Life*. The target paper reported that a PhiID-derived quantity, Phi-r, showed punctuated dynamics before self-replication, was associated with and higher during replication, predicted later replication from the first quarter of a trajectory and could guide molecular interventions in the Graded Autocatalysis Replication Domain (GARD), a stochastic model in which catalytic lipid-like molecular assemblies exchange components, grow and divide by fission.

**Foundation established (S01–S11R).** The AI Scientist decomposed the paper into 59 claims, catalogued 105 implementation choices requiring explicit assumptions, recorded 12 cross-text or cross-source tensions and created a 120-parameter branch registry; pinned the paper and public GARD/PhiID source lineages; built historical-reference and independent GARD engines; froze deterministic seed and trajectory schemas; and validated stochastic laws, fission, labels, compositional transforms and eligible PhiID backends. The named simulator branches were reproducible, but none could be identified as the unavailable author implementation, label definitions in the available materials led to materially different reconstructions, one optional discrete OmegaID path failed relabel invariance, and our proposed 32–256-observation Phi windows failed the estimator-validation gate.

**Future-information use was flagged as a temporal-scope risk in our reconstruction (S08, S11).** The S08 label-family audit noted that retrospective label configurations use later observations, built a separately identified past-only companion for each graph family and observed 21 label flips on diagnostic fixtures. S11 accordingly marked whole-trajectory Phi estimates `NON_PROSPECTIVE_WHOLE_TRAJECTORY_DESCRIPTION`. This was an early methodological suspicion about pipelines we were constructing, not evidence that the authors' analysis leaked future information or that their reported result was wrong.

### 2026-08-02

**Strict minimal replication and source audit (S12–S12E).** Twelve strict baseline trajectories produced negative prospective Phi–replication associations in every registered summary, and only one of 1,090 treated intervention opportunities had a distinguishable Phi-directed action. Public IIGR and PhiRL source semantics were reconstructed using documented compatibility adaptations, but neither public scalar yielded a coherent positive prospective result in our pipelines; the source-defined `emergence = synergy + downward causation` identity was verified, while historical, paper-described and public-source simulation clocks could not be mapped uniquely under the unscaled interpretations we tested.

### 2026-08-03

**Paper-time inference remained nonidentifiable (S12F–S12G).** Approximate time-base inference recovered three nonunique simulator contracts whose trajectory lengths resembled the paper's visible molecular-time axis, but daughter choice, exposure, overshoot and recording semantics could not be uniquely inferred. Candidate-ensemble execution then failed a preregistered operational gate, so no favorable scientific result was promoted.

### 2026-08-05

**Boundary-clock and waiver checks failed closed (S12H–S12I).** A boundary-inclusive candidate nearly matched the paper's time envelope but missed the frozen aggregate-support gate, and a human-authorized sensitivity analysis still failed its complete validation contract. These runs narrowed plausible clocks without identifying the author pipeline.

### 2026-08-06

**Aggregation repair and held-out scale-up did not support the source-informed hypothesis (S12J–S13RRR).** The aggregation interface was repaired and candidate schemas were normalized, but the clean held-out two-candidate scale-up remained non-supportive. Candidates 2 and 3 were retained as explicit, paper-time-compatible reconstructions rather than estimates of author identity.

### 2026-08-07

**Future dependence was confirmed in one reconstructed analysis, not in the authors' pipeline (S13X–S13Y).** An adaptive search found that completed-trajectory PhiRL source-defined emergence was positively associated with the adjacent-composition label `Y = I(H>0.9)`, and a frozen confirmation on 100 new catalytic matrices shared across both candidates—200 trajectories in total—reproduced that direction. Completed-trajectory fitting produced 73/100 and 79/99 positive tasks, whereas past-only prefix refitting gave median correlations of −0.062/−0.072, so this particular lead was classified `LABEL_COUPLED_RETROSPECTIVE_RESEMBLANCE`, not early warning. The finding established temporal dependence in S13X's reconstruction, but neither tested the authors' unavailable implementation nor showed that future-information use explained their reported result.

**Our reconstructions of the target paper's Figures 2–5 were assessed (S14–S16).** Individual Phi-like trajectories displayed punctuated excursions and temporal dependence, but our reconstructed aggregate trend was significantly positive rather than the paper's reported null. Reconstructions of Figures 3–4 had partial directional resemblance only under completed fitting, while in our Figure 5 reconstruction the target was positive about 98% of the time, its majority baseline was therefore about 98% rather than the visible paper value near 60%, and balanced prediction remained at chance with no qualifying pre-onset run at the first-quarter cutoff.

### 2026-08-08

**Phi-directed causal control was not reproduced in our reconstruction and the original E01 assessment closed (S17–S18).** Our literal reading of the online append-edit-refit procedure was executable and exactly replayable, but the reported max/control/min ordering, action separation and stronger paired-control expectations were not supported in our implementations. The frozen 59-claim ledger became 3 `SUPPORTED`, 17 `DIRECTIONALLY_SUPPORTED`, 21 `NOT_SUPPORTED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`, 2 `UNDERDETERMINED` and 16 `NOT_EVALUATED`: partial directional retrospective reconstruction, with prospective prediction and causal control unsupported in the tested public-source pipelines. Because target-specific code had not yet been published, this assessment could not adjudicate the authors' exact implementation.

**Additive discovery search began (S19-L01–L05).** Without changing S18, the AI Scientist reopened the scientific search around alternative replicator definitions, compotypes, boundary inheritance and cross-generation recurrence. Early label variants changed occupancy and onset but did not jointly reproduce the paper's persistence, probability, consistency and first-arrival fingerprints.

### 2026-08-09

**A high-occupancy paper-like label proved nonunique and failed untouched reproduction (S19-L07–L08).** Exploratory boundary-only and high-Poisson-exposure definitions each approached the paper-like 88% occupancy—87.72–87.77% at the fission boundary and 88.03–88.38% at high exposure—so the match did not identify a unique mechanism. On 100 new matched matrices, boundary-only occupancy was 0.8581/0.8608, high-exposure occupancy 0.8750/0.8698 and the projection alternative 0.8470/0.8457; only four of six joint gates passed, yielding `NEITHER_MECHANISM_REPRODUCES_ON_UNTOUCHED_DATA`.

**The most direct public-source label and metric reconstructions were tested (S19-L06–L12).** Past-only multi-attractor, occupancy, MATLAB-compatible recurring-attractor and all-compotype-union branches either failed closed or failed untouched confirmation. Our paper–PhiRL concordance audit could not identify a unique mapping among the equation, prose, figures and public-source outputs; target-specific code was required to distinguish the possible complete pipelines.

### 2026-08-10

**Figure 5 and related-source alternatives did not yield prospective prediction in our reconstructions (S19-L13–L17).** Recurring-attractor targets could reproduce the visible majority-baseline prevalence, and padding or sequence length could inflate all-cell accuracy, but valid-cell models remained nondiscriminative and no source-supported tensor or architecture reproduced the complete panel in our candidates. A related `BreakingGRNMemories` Phi lineage also did not produce an unchanged, independent signal in both reconstructed GARD candidates.

### 2026-08-11

**Broad early-warning searches were negative, but a real committor was found (S19-L18–L31).** Critical-slowing, recurrence, topology, geometry, survival, random-feature, event-aligned, local-operator and transition-tube representations did not add robust prospective value. Repeated stochastic shooting nevertheless showed that restored states had a highly reliable finite-horizon probability of entering a completed-lineage attractor, and an eight-step selected-clock propagator coordinate predicted that probability on untouched matrices; this was a genuine simulator signal conditioned on a retrospectively chosen destination.

**The destination's retrospective origin was flagged from the first committor loop (S19-L28–L31).** L28 froze the completed-run L23 target centroid as the basin and labeled it explicitly `RETROSPECTIVE_COMPLETED_RUN_MATRIX_SPECIFIC`, and every following loop repeated that the target basin remained retrospective and completed-run matrix-specific and could not establish online early warning. The committor was real, but the place it pointed to had been chosen by looking at a completed trajectory, making this a separate limitation of the target definition rather than evidence about the target paper's analysis.

**The destination failed to transfer and the project pivoted from places to processes (S19-L32–L42).** Static distillation attempts did not robustly recover the shooting teacher, and independent lineages under the same catalytic matrix did not share one transferable basin or attractor atlas. Process-level tests then separated ordinary persistence, inheritance breaks, generic resumption and strict return to an old composition: renewal after disruption was common, whereas exact old-neighbourhood recovery was extremely rare.

**The process idea's vocabulary predated its necessity (S08, S19-L02–L05, S19-L21).** Every ingredient of the process view existed before the destination programme failed. S08 had defined a past-only continuous recurrence diagnostic `R_g = max_{h<g} H(n_g, n_h)` — similarity of the current composition to its own history — and deliberately saved it unthresholded. L04 then defined replicator membership as strict `H>0.9` recurrence to a state in a different generation at sequence separation greater than one, so that location alone could not qualify, and L05 made that definition strictly past-only, which later made a recurrence pattern usable as a prospective target. L21 modelled attractor onset as discrete-time hazards, establishing the survival task from which the final L48–L54 state-local duration-hazard teacher descends, although the reformulation itself was non-support. When L31–L36 eliminated every static and place-based alternative, this accumulated vocabulary was the only viable target language left, and L37 wrote it down.

**Four specific results built up to the process idea (S19-L31–L36).** L31's untouched confirmation meant the one reliable object was already process-shaped: a probability measured by rerunning the simulator from a restored state, not a static property of that state. Three consecutive distillation attempts then failed to capture it in any snapshot — the observed 32-state prefix tube (L32, `CONFIRMED_COMMITTOR_NOT_RECOVERED_BY_FROZEN_OBSERVED_PREFIX_TUBES`), a basin-blind operator-memory and phase compression (L33) and the complete current physical state with the full catalytic graph (L34, `COMPACT_BETA_GRAPH_SIGNATURE_MISSES_SHOOTING_SIGNAL`). L35's mechanism attribution showed why: entrant and nonentrant branches separate only after stochastic propagation begins — its report states the contrasts "cannot show that the same information is measurable at the starting state" — and the teacher's dominant coefficient was the short-branch ensemble probability itself (`NO_UNIVERSAL_SHORT_BRANCH_MECHANISM`). L36 then asked point-blank whether the basin was a reproducible network property or a trajectory-specific object reconstructed from the same completed lineage being explained: every independently seeded reference lineage formed its own recurring basin (eligible fraction 1.0, at-risk fractions 0.94–1.0), but strict `H>0.9` centroid agreement was only 0.60–0.75 and every transfer and stability gate failed (`TARGET_BASIN_LINEAGE_SPECIFIC`). Regime formation recurred universally while regime identity did not — the phenomenon was a pattern, not a place.

**The pattern-instead-of-destination idea first appeared inside L37's own report (S19-L37–L38).** L36's recommendation was still destination-shaped, but after reciprocal held-out tests returned `MULTILINEAGE_ATTRACTOR_FAMILY_NOT_SUPPORTED` and `TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC_TARGET_CONFIRMED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`, L37's handoff recommended `PAST_ONLY_RECURRENCE_INHERITANCE_OUTCOME_CONSTRUCTION` — the first written proposal to replace a place-shaped target with a past-observable recurrence–inheritance event. L38 executed it, asking whether a target that needs no completed test trajectory has a reliable state-dependent probability, with the event defined structurally as a selected post-fission daughter with strict `H>0.9` heredity to its parent and to an earlier inherited daughter. Its verdict `INDEPENDENT_EVENT_TARGET_REQUIRES_REDEFINITION` sent the definition through the L39–L47 refinements that became plastic heredity and, later, the F12 break-and-renewal process-risk target.

### 2026-08-12

**Plastic heredity was identified (S19-L43–L47).** Similarity to the old pre-break composition fell by about 0.26 when heredity resumed, contradicting fixed-composition restoration. A seven-outcome audit found common breaks, frequent renewed three- and five-fission inheritance episodes and modest temporal stickiness, while old return remained near zero; past-only PhiID added no value beyond direct heredity controls, and apparent functional coherence did not survive controls for composition and chronology.

**A past-observable process-risk coordinate was discovered and untouched-confirmed (S19-L48–L54).** The target became an F12 inheritance break followed by three consecutive inherited fissions. Shooting established a reliable state-dependent probability, state-local duration hazards supplied a branch-derived teacher, and L53 distilled it into a frozen target-blind full-state graph-plus-history model. L54 applied that model without refitting to 40 new shared matrices, 80 trajectories, 400 restored states and 25,600 independent futures plus exact regeneration; split-half reliability was 0.938/0.924, frozen overall ranks were about 0.895–0.918, within-matrix ranks about 0.550–0.697, and every registered proper-score, permutation, availability and replay gate passed in both candidates.

### 2026-08-13

**E01 closed deterministically (S20).** S20 added no scientific outcome, preserved S18 byte-for-byte, recorded L54 separately as a confirmed simulator-process result and produced the discovery note, handover, claim addendum and V3 evidence bundle. The separately authorized E02 first stage was documented but not executed inside E01; the later Codex and Fable programs are independent clean-room branches, not continuations disguised as E01/E02 results. The exact boundary was that L54 supported a past-observable precursor for plastic-heredity break-and-renewal, not PhiID, paper replication, first-replicator prediction, fixed-composition homeostasis, intervention efficacy or evidence about real chemistry.

## Robert Bjarnason working on replications with Codex Sol and Claude Fable

### 2026-08-13

**Independent Codex replication reproduced the qualitative core.** A clean-room implementation reproduced reliable state-dependent F12 break-and-renewal probability and the frozen composite model's advantage over direct history at the original 40-matrix scale and again with 200 development and 200 confirmation matrices. Exact numerical agreement with the Eidosoma signature was incomplete, but branch reliability, within-matrix ranking, proper-score gain and exact replay remained positive at fivefold scale.

**Codex adversarial tests narrowed mechanism and memory claims.** A first prospective ablation appeared to support current state and beta-conditioned state, but a stricter provenance-complete, no-PCA confirmation on 200 new matrices failed every complete state, static-beta and beta-conditioned-interaction gate, leaving the successful composite algorithm mechanistically unresolved. Review also withdrew Eidosoma L44's `0.015–0.022 bits/transition` Markov estimate because its IID model was fitted and scored on different supports; a new prospective 32-fission MEMCONF analysis instead found support-matched Markov-over-IID gains of 0.046953/0.033936 bits per transition and duration-over-Markov gains of 0.010770/0.009984, all with Holm-adjusted `p=0.000976`, while explicitly rejecting biological-memory, molecular-storage and causal interpretations.

**Codex showed that the three-fission target was not a coherent regime.** Replaying 145,516 qualifying episodes across three confirmation cohorts found that only 4.5–6.5% placed every daughter pair above `H=0.9`; most episodes were far from the old anchor and often persisted locally, but adjacent inheritance was not transitive. The supported language was narrowed from “regime switching” to plastic-heredity break-and-renewal.

**Independent Fable replication reproduced and scaled the qualitative core.** Fable implemented separate clean-room simulator contracts, reproduced the L54-style result at 40 matrices, repeated it at 200 matrices and then at 1,000 development plus 1,000 confirmation matrices, with exact replay and stable proper-score and rank gains. Its beta-only model increasingly captured between-matrix propensity while remaining near zero within matrices, sharpening the distinction between matrix-level capacity and state-local risk.

**Fable independently rejected a coherent three-fission regime interpretation.** In a prospective upgrade, two of three coherence gates failed in both candidates: only 6.0–7.2% of joint events were mutually coherent, coherent-target reliability was 0.320/0.274 and span similarity was about 0.72. This independently demoted “regime switching” to the supported break-and-renewal/hereditary-capacity description.

**Fable independently repaired the inheritance-sequence comparator.** Reanalysis on common transition support confirmed that the IID fitting bug was real but contributed only about 0.0009/0.0006 bits per transition in Fable's clean-room data. Corrected Markov-over-IID gains remained 0.0614/0.0399 bits with bootstrap intervals excluding zero; heterogeneous-matrix IID controls exposed a separate roughly 0.004-bit null floor, leaving a large but explicitly non-mechanistic dependence signal rather than evidence of biological memory.

**Fable isolated a candidate mechanism within its branch.** Fable's ablations and deduplicated registry-v2 model attributed the predictive advantage primarily to catalytic-network-conditioned current state plus unique history, found robust target-threshold sensitivity and near calibration, and identified a weak-catalytic-heterogeneity boundary at `(A, sigma)=(-4,3)`. This branch-specific mechanism was later placed in tension with Codex's no-PCA null and therefore remained a priority for independent replication.

**Fable established causal molecular control of the F12 process.** On fresh states, mass-preserving swaps selected by the frozen v2 coordinate shifted realized break-and-renewal probability by about +0.156/+0.158 between up and down arms, while matched random swaps did nothing; intermediate edits produced a graded dose response. The effect transferred to three perturbed regimes and vanished in the preregistered weak-heterogeneity null regime, and a one-scalar outgoing-catalytic-influence rule retained about 65% of the learned controller's effect.

**Fable extended one-shot control to closed-loop heredity steering.** Per-fission score-selected swaps increased plastic turnover in the up direction and produced near-continuous heredity in the down direction, whereas random editing tracked no-op. Down-steering made assemblies concentrated, catalytically active and convergent across lineages under continued control; subsequent decomposition showed the intervention primarily altered break resistance, with renewal quality also moving.

**Fable found that control maintains rather than installs an assembly-level attractor.** Steer–release–challenge experiments showed that written compositions decayed toward the natural drift floor within roughly 5–10 fissions and had no detectable cross-generation basin or dose-dependent return. The controller therefore maintained an assembly state while active rather than installing a self-restoring molecular identity.

**A preliminary novelty search bounded the discovery claim.** Searches found extensive prior art for GARD compositional inheritance, composomes/compotypes, catalytic-network prediction, transferable heredity measures, PhiID/causal-emergence formalisms and committor estimation, but no directly matching publication for the full prospective break-and-renewal, stochastic-shooting and untouched-transfer combination. This negative search was explicitly not proof of priority, could miss differently worded or unpublished work, and initially had only abstract-level access to a key Kahana comparator later addressed directly in the cross-clock phase.

### 2026-08-14

**Fable reconciled competing attractor results in simulated catalytic lipid-like assemblies.** Phase E found no fixed cross-generational basin for controller-written compositions, apparently conflicting with Amit Kahana, Lior Segev and Doron Lancet's 2023 *Attractor dynamics drives self-reproduction in protobiological catalytic networks*. Phase F first reproduced their historical GARD10 protocol—94–97% of random starts reached a composome—then showed why both results hold: growth attracts assemblies toward a phase-conditioned composome set, whereas fission, drift and multistable switching prevent return to one permanent composition. A validated positive-control basin assay and convergence of independent lineages toward a matrix-specific distribution over composomes supported a distributional attractor rather than a fixed molecular self. This branch-specific result offers a possible bridge in the long-running GARD heredity-and-evolvability debate by separating genuine composome-level attraction from durable individual identity, while not itself testing selection or open-ended evolution.

**Codex prospectively confirmed a stricter coherent episode but not its first predictor.** A new F32 endpoint required a break followed by eight inherited fissions whose daughters were all mutually `H>0.9` and all `H<=0.85` from the old anchor. The event occurred in both candidates and branch halves at 1.81–2.11% of futures with exact replay, but the registered state-added model passed only one of four prediction cells, establishing occurrence without robust prospective prediction, recurrence, attractor identity or control.

**Codex's six-family strict-event pilot stopped at its registered gate.** Eighty new matrices, 800 restored states and 102,400 exactly replayed F32 futures provided adequate event power, and four model families improved over history/clocks in all four development cells. Model selection was unstable between direct ridge and a staged hurdle model—43.63% versus 55.44% of bootstrap wins—so neither reached the required 75%, no model was frozen, and the original confirmation path was correctly blocked.

**Fable Phase G found no state-specific competency attractor and separated resistance from resilience.** Behavioral fingerprints relaxed toward each catalytic matrix's typical repertoire rather than returning to a written state's own behavior. Identical-state interventions independently moved break resistance and near-ceiling post-break renewal, but an explore–recover–consolidate controller failed its novelty gate because effective consolidation suppressed natural episode turnover, exposing a stability–novelty tradeoff.

**Fable found accumulating hysteresis and quantified feedback cost.** Longer stabilization pulses increased post-release decay half-life from roughly five to roughly fourteen fissions, revising the simpler claim that written depth leaves no trace while still falling short of an attractor. Periodic control degraded gracefully, and the lowest tested successful event-triggered setting maintained about 0.975 inheritance with roughly 16–17 informed edits per 60 fissions.

**Fable's mechanism tournament favored catalytic-web tightness.** Disagreement tests eliminated flux alignment as the driver where it opposed the learned model and influence rule, transplantation supplied partial relational network-by-state evidence, and norm-matched beta surgery at fixed composition causally shifted break risk while random beta edits did not. Together with the molecular-swap results, Fable identified outgoing catalytic-web influence as a causally sufficient branch-specific dial.

**Fable's internalization ladder was anti-monotone.** A memoryless one-scalar influence rule applied every generation recovered 92–96% of the full model's maintenance gain and transferred better than more information-rich gated or distilled policies; the trained model's remaining advantage was chiefly deciding when to act. A soft kinetic retention-bias prototype was null at the registered strengths, suggesting that the actuator, rather than the compact policy description, was the internalization bottleneck.

**Fable independently replicated the strict eight-fission occurrence and closed its branch.** Under a sealed endpoint and fresh 200-matrix cohort, Fable generated 256,000 F32 futures plus exact replay and observed rates of 1.70–2.00% across four cells; every confidence interval contained the corresponding Codex rate and 121–127 of 200 matrices contributed events. This independently confirmed the rare coherent, old-anchor-distinct episode as an occurrence phenomenon, not its prediction, control, recurrence or attractor status.

### 2026-08-15

**Codex converted the failed pilot's post-hoc ensemble idea into a new prospective test.** The pilot remained a registered failure, while a separate protocol froze the unweighted probability average of its direct and hurdle models, prohibited refitting or recalibration and generated 200 untouched matrices, 2,000 states and 256,000 F32 futures plus complete exact replay.

**The Codex ensemble did not pass the all-candidate confirmation gate.** The frozen ensemble improved log loss directionally in all four cells and passed both candidate-03 halves, but both candidate-02 confidence intervals crossed zero and multiplicity-adjusted tests failed; the preregistered overall verdict was therefore `primary_prediction_supported: false`. The strict episode itself occurred again at about 1.71% in candidate 02 and 2.12% in candidate 03, reinforcing occurrence while leaving transferable state-based prediction unresolved.

**Codex was redirected to independently replicate Fable's causal discoveries.** With strict-eight predictor search explicitly closed, a new clean-room directive targeted the already validated F12 `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3` process using Codex's own simulator contracts, frozen predictor, states, seeds and code. It prohibited importing Fable artifacts, pooling candidates or rescuing the failed strict predictor, and specified serial sealed tests of one-shot molecular control, graded dose response, a simple catalytic-support rule, fixed-composition beta surgery, resistance versus resilience, parameter transfer and a predicted null, closed-loop steering, release/challenge, hysteresis, feedback cost and exploratory internalization. At this evidence cutoff the directive was a protocol, not a scientific result.

**Codex reproduced model-guided one-molecule control at developmental scale.** On 40 fresh catalytic matrices, 400 restored states and 51,200 F12 futures plus complete deterministic replay, swaps chosen by the frozen Codex predictor shifted `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3` probability by 0.0931–0.1031 between model-up and model-down in all four candidate/half cells; every whole-matrix confidence interval excluded zero and every Holm-adjusted value was `0.000976`. Random-minus-no-op point contrasts remained within the registered `±0.025` pilot margin and pilot eligibility passed, independently reproducing Fable's central one-shot control direction, although the stronger full four-cell gate failed in two cells and an untouched molecular confirmation had not yet been run at that stage.

**Codex recovered Fable's compact outgoing-influence rule after an orientation correction.** The sealed P2 test of incoming support, `beta @ x`, was negative and was retained as a valid negative control rather than misclassified as a failed Fable replication; post-seal clarification established that Fable's rule used outgoing source influence, `x @ beta`. A fresh P2b cohort then produced outgoing-rule up-minus-down effects of 0.0831–0.0972 in all four cells, with positive whole-matrix intervals, Holm-adjusted `p=0.000976` and exact replay. The causal direction therefore reproduced, but the registered pilot-eligibility and full gates remained false because one random-minus-no-op point contrast exceeded the `±0.025` margin and the complete specificity conditions did not pass in every cell.

**Codex reproduced the targeted beta-surgery effect and established graded two-dose ordering.** The first sealed P3 experiment used a much smaller 5% Frobenius perturbation and was null in candidate 02 and weak in candidate 03; after Fable clarified that its actual frozen intervention multiplied the occupied catalytic block by `1.5` or divided it by `1.5`, Codex preregistered P3b on 80 new matrices, 960 states and 215,040 F12 futures per pass. Fable-strength loosen-minus-tighten effects were 0.0891–0.1242 at landmark 60 and 0.0950–0.1222 across five other landmarks, with positive intervals, Holm-adjusted `p=0.000976` and exact replay, and the stronger dose exceeded the 5% dose in every registered cell. The formal Fable-strength replication and five-landmark gates nevertheless remained false because the matched random present-block surgery was not equivalent to no-op, so the targeted causal contrast and dose ordering reproduced while specificity against an equally sized random network perturbation remained unresolved.

**Codex diagnosed why the P3b random-control gate failed without rewriting its verdict.** A sealed audit using no new matrices or futures showed that the balanced-log random surgery was random in edge direction but not neutral in launch-state catalytic throughput: it strengthened the occupied web on average, and its outcome shift followed the same inverse throughput–break-and-renewal relation seen across the frozen P3b arms. This explained why the matched random arm did not behave like no-op and motivated a fresh exact-throughput-neutral intervention, but the audit did not rescue or relabel P3b's failed specificity gate.

**A prospective catalytic-throughput program reproduced the strength effect but showed that topology also matters.** A 40-matrix P3c pilot passed its primary gate, after which a separately authorized untouched confirmation used 160 fresh matrices, 1,920 restored states and 307,200 F12 futures plus exact replay. Loosening versus tightening the occupied catalytic web shifted `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3` by 0.1034–0.1134 across the four cells, with negative throughput slopes and rank associations and Holm-adjusted `p=0.000976` throughout; the registered F6 first-break contrasts were likewise 0.1141–0.1231. The omnibus F12 and resistance gates nevertheless remained false because an exactly launch-throughput-neutral topology perturbation changed F12 by 0.0103–0.0234 and first-break probability by 0.0258–0.0355 rather than behaving like no-op. The resulting causal interpretation is narrower and stronger: catalytic strength is a robust control axis, but the scalar `x^T beta x` is not a complete description because the arrangement of the occupied catalytic web also changes growth and break dynamics.

**Codex independently reproduced causal recovery from an identical natural inheritance break.** P4 first waited for an untreated lineage to break, saved the exact selected daughter and restored that same post-break state across every arm, avoiding conditioning on treatment-created breaks. The sealed cohort contained 805 eligible states from 124 candidate-02 and 125 candidate-03 matrices and generated 128,800 primary futures plus 128,800 exact replay futures. Tightening rather than loosening the occupied catalytic web increased subsequent three-fission recovery by 0.0337–0.0491 in all four cells, with every 95% whole-matrix lower bound above zero and every Holm-adjusted value `p=0.000976`; an exactly throughput-neutral topology perturbation independently reduced recovery by 0.0263–0.0375 in all four cells. This reproduces Fable's central resilience direction while showing that catalytic strength and network arrangement are separable causal contributors; it does not establish biological repair, memory, agency or an autonomous attractor.

**The tested Fable causal program is now directionally reproduced across the independent Codex implementation.** Every Fable causal direction tested so far recurred under Codex's own simulator contracts, matrices, states, seeds and code: predictor-guided molecular swaps, the correctly oriented outgoing-influence rule, graded occupied-block catalytic strength, first-break resistance and identical-break recovery. This is a full directional replication of the tested one-shot mechanism and resistance–resilience program, not a claim that every preregistered omnibus gate passed: random-control specificity exposed reproducible topology effects, and failed gates remain part of the result rather than being hidden. Fable's transfer, closed-loop steering, release/challenge, hysteresis, feedback-cost, distributional-attractor and internalization findings are not yet independently reproduced by Codex at this cutoff.

**Codex's full-scale molecular confirmation passed the complete registered gate.** The prospectively frozen CR1 campaign completed on 200 entirely fresh catalytic matrices, 2,000 restored states and 512,000 primary F12 futures plus a complete 512,000-future replay. Without refitting, recalibration or threshold changes, the immutable predictor exhaustively selected the highest- and lowest-scored legal one-molecule substitutions, with random substitution and no-op controls receiving common future streams. Model-up minus model-down was 0.1071–0.1233 across the four candidate/half cells; model-up exceeded no-op by 0.0354–0.0454 and no-op exceeded model-down by 0.0717–0.0780. Every 95% whole-matrix lower bound was positive, every Holm-adjusted sign-randomization value was `p=0.000976`, and random-minus-no-op was equivalent within the registered `±0.025` margin and no larger than 25% of the main effect in every cell. All seven cell-level conditions therefore passed `4/4`; all twenty landmark-specific up-minus-down contrasts were positive, state-centred predicted-versus-realized slopes were 0.523–0.604, all 2,000 states replayed exactly and complete artifact readback passed.
