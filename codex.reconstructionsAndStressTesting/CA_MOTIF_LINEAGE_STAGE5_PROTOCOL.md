# Preregistered CA motif-lineage Stage 5: physical localization

## Question and ancestry

Stage 4 freshly confirmed that a 64-bit, 16-coordinate Walsh payload can carry
and renew lineage-specific form through a bitwise-identical visible reset. Stage
5 asks a narrower and harder question: can those same 16 coordinates live in a
strictly local, transported cellular field, enter a daughter through a small
spatial bottleneck, spread only by nearest-neighbour transport, guide visible
development locally, and be rewritten locally by that development?

The registered Stage-4 winner is `walsh-r016-q04`. Its basis, channel order,
four-bit quantizers, reader strength, writer window, reset board, rule, scores,
and causal thresholds are frozen. The exact archived arrays are loaded; they
are not refitted. Stage-5 exploratory work may use only Stage-4-exposed pairs.
The first 128 of Stage 4's 158 untouched reserve pairs form the sealed Stage-5
confirmation cohort. The remaining 30 stay untouched for a later audit.

This is a synthetic CA experiment. A positive result establishes neither life,
agency, metabolism, nor a nonphysical carrier. No Wagner or Fable source code
may be read, imported, hashed, or executed.

## Local carrier

Each visible 16x16 torus is accompanied by a signed 16x16x16 field: 16 Walsh
channels at every lattice site. Every channel uses the frozen Stage-4 scale;
the working field is float32 and the frozen exact-zero four-bit quantizer is
applied only to the boundary patch. This large developmental field is not the
inherited payload. At a generation boundary only a registered spatial patch is
retained; every other field value is exactly zero. The primary patch is one
site, hence 16 four-bit values or 64 inherited bits. Two-by-two and four-by-four
patches (256 and 1,024 inherited bits) are registered fallbacks. A uniform
global broadcast is an explicitly nonlocal upper-bound diagnostic.

The inherited patch location is selected deterministically from pair and
future identifiers, independently of lineage. Translation assays move it by a
registered offset on the torus. The visible daughter is always reset to the
same bitwise-identical board before every generation.

Only nearest-neighbour transport can move field information. One transport
step mixes a site's value with the mean of its eight Moore neighbours. A local
writer sees only the current site's 3x3 visible motif and its current field.
For each selected Walsh channel that motif supplies a parity sign. Writing is
an affine, label-blind update followed by frozen four-bit quantization. The
analytic writer uses the Stage-4 channel scale as its slope and zero intercept.
The affine writer is fitted without lineage labels from exposed Stage-3R
carrier traces: each trace is converted into its implied motif distribution,
and an independent through-data affine map predicts each archived Walsh
coefficient from the corresponding motif parity moment.

The reader is equally local. At each visible site it reconstructs motif-energy
differences from the 16 field values found at the affected motif sites; it has
no access to a spatial mean, lineage label, parent state, target form, or future
outcome. Reading occurs during frozen sweeps 1--32. Writing occurs only during
frozen sweeps 49--64. The generation contains 64 sweeps and process noise
0.002. The daughter rewrites its own field for the next boundary.

## Registered mechanics and calibration

Before outcome-bearing screening, an outcome-blind transport calibration
crosses diffusion 0.05/0.10/0.20/0.35, write gain 0.08/0.16/0.32, and retention
0.50/0.75/1.00 for both analytic and affine writers. Synthetic impulses,
uniform fields, and motif mosaics score transport reach, stability,
reconstructability, saturation, and finite values. No lineage labels or
phenotype outcomes enter this score. The top two parameter triples per writer,
constrained to distinct diffusion values, advance. Each is crossed with the
three local bottlenecks and the nonlocal
broadcast anchor, capped at 16 candidates by deterministic score, inherited
bits, and identifier order.

The exposed-pair screen uses 64 pairs, 16 futures per history, and eight
generations. At most four local candidates plus the global anchor advance.
Qualification uses 96 exposed pairs, 32 futures per history, and 16
generations. The smoke and pilot profiles reduce counts but never change
mechanics or thresholds.

## Spectral anatomy and interventions

The anatomy and qualification phases together measure the full 16-mode payload, all 16
leave-one-mode-out lesions, all 16 single-mode carriers, 32 deterministic
balanced eight-mode masks, eight D4 motif orientations, equal-scale channels,
and half-channel recombinations (the latter in qualification). This maps whether success is concentrated in
one mode, distributed across modes, orientation-specific, or dependent on
channel identity. Anatomy can rank candidates but cannot alter the frozen
Walsh basis or quantizer.

Qualification applies the complete causal ladder:

- intact local renewal;
- zero every boundary;
- channel shuffle and spatial shuffle every boundary;
- read disabled, write disabled, no rewrite, and transport disabled;
- ablation after generation 2;
- correct and opposite contemporaneous rescue entering generation 4;
- opposite founder history;
- one-percent channel sign corruption;
- patch translation, half-width bottleneck, and dispersed equal-bit placement;
- half-channel recombination and its complement.

Ordinary, moderate, and harsh damage are preregistered. Moderate damage combines
10 percent retained-value erasure, five percent sign corruption, and process
noise 0.004. Harsh damage combines 25 percent erasure, ten percent sign
corruption, and process noise 0.008. Damage is applied only to inherited field
values, not to the shared codebook or visible reset.

A local candidate must first pass the frozen Stage-3R strict renewal ladder. It
must additionally show positive, interval-supported intact advantages over
channel shuffle and transport disabled, retain at least half its intact effect
under translation, obey the causal light cone, and pass the registered payload
and locality audit. Selection and qualification may nominate no more than
three finalists: the 64-bit one-site candidate if qualified, the smallest
qualified local fallback, and the strongest qualified local candidate.

## Transfer, confirmation, and verdicts

Frozen finalists are mapped without retuning on the already-exposed rule-31648
and rule-70366 panels. These maps are exploratory only.

Confirmation is a separate invocation requiring `--resume` and
`--authorize-confirmation`. It opens all 128 sealed pairs, uses 64 futures per
history and 16 generations, and tests at most three objects: the nonlocal
Stage-4 Walsh anchor, the registered one-site local candidate, and the smallest
qualified fallback. Each receives the full causal ladder under ordinary and
moderate damage. Pair-cluster bootstrap intervals use 10,000 resamples and
alpha 0.005 per object. Nothing may be refitted or renominated after this gate.

Verdicts are hierarchical:

- `ROBUST_LOCAL_64BIT_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: the one-site, 64-bit local
  field passes ordinarily and under moderate damage;
- `LOCAL_64BIT_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: it passes ordinarily only;
- `LOCAL_DISTRIBUTED_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: a two- or four-site local
  bottleneck passes ordinarily;
- `GLOBAL_BROADCAST_ONLY`: only the nonlocal Walsh upper bound passes;
- `SPECTRAL_STRUCTURE_NOT_LOCALIZED`: exposed anatomy is informative but no
  fresh local object passes;
- `NO_STAGE4_REPLICATION`: the frozen nonlocal anchor fails freshly.

The report separately counts inherited payload bits, developmental field
storage, and shared codebook/mechanism bits. It never presents the 131,072-bit
float32 developmental working field as a 64-bit object.

## Execution and artifacts

Reference invocations use at most 20 workers and eight wall hours, reserving 30
minutes for orderly checkpointing. The phases are `audit`, `anatomy`,
`calibrate`, `transcode`, `localize`, `qualify`, `transfer`, `adjudicate`, and
`confirm`. All random streams are semantic, paired across histories and
independent across futures. Checkpoints, design/model/cohort hashes, status,
ETA, PID, logs, reports, and queue state are atomic and resume-bound.
Confirmation never launches automatically, and Stage 6 never launches from
Stage 5.
