# CA motif-lineage Stage 6B-R protocol

## Status and claim boundary

Stage 6A-R is immutable evidence. Its corrected whole-lattice carrier retained
strong plastic heredity, while no radius-at-most-five candidate survived the
registered generation-8 screen. Post-screen diagnostics showed a narrower and
more specific failure: radius-five carriers were effective in generation one,
active rewriting retained a positive effect through generation four, and the
signal amplitude survived, but carrier identity rotated toward chance by
generation eight. Founder clamping restored recovery. Stage 6B-R therefore
tests renewal stability, causal coverage, and scaling in a separate design.

This is engineered synthetic cellular-automaton heredity. It is not a claim
about natural cells, organisms, or human memory. No Wagner or Fable source code
may be read, imported, hashed, or executed.

## Frozen operation

The reference campaign uses rule 31649, matched visible-state reset before
every generation, 64 visible sweeps, process noise 0.002, read sweeps 1--32,
write sweeps 49--64, and observation sweeps 57--64. Random streams are paired
by semantic purpose and do not depend on candidate labels, ordering, worker
count, or resume boundaries.

The campaign has a hard maximum of four workers and one persisted eight-hour
wall deadline. It runs as five checkpointed stages. Developmental expansion
ends no later than 6.25 hours so that 1.75 hours remain reserved for
adjudication. A negative stage is recorded rather than repaired in place.

## 1. Transient local heredity

The three frozen radius-five generation-4 near-misses are tested on the 96
exposed locality-qualification pairs at generations 1, 2, 4, and 6. Conditions
are intact, founder-clamped, no-rewrite, write-disabled, transport-disabled,
communication-cut, zero, shuffle, opposite-founder, and translated.

Transient local PH requires generation-4 crossover at least 0.15 with a
positive 97.5-percent pair-bootstrap lower bound, both lineage directions
positive, at least half of pair effects positive, survival at least 0.90,
positive active-rewrite advantage, loss under targeted causal controls, and at
least 70-percent translation retention. This verdict is independent of later
durability gates.

## 2. Renewal repair

Radius-five single-seed carriers cross five writer geometries, three shared
writer fits, and four turnover values. Writer geometries are the frozen
directed 5x5 and 8x8 rectangles plus centred 5x5, 9x9, and 11x11 squares. The
centred 11x11 writer is wholly inside a co-located radius-five light cone.

The inherited slow latch is

`S[g+1] = Q(rho*S[g] + (1-rho)*W[g])`,

for rho in 0, 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75. Its no-write control is `Q(rho*S[g])`, so
passive persistence cannot satisfy the active-renewal gate.

Writers are: the frozen global ridge; a geometry-specific ridge fitted to
generation-one local moments; and a closed-loop ridge refitted three times to
pooled generations 1--4 from its own rollouts. All fits are shared across
families. Labels and targets are fit-time only and are inaccessible at runtime.

The 60 candidates are calibrated on a frozen 32-pair subset. Six diverse
nominees are tested with and without systematic Hamming(8,4) protection for
each four-bit carrier channel on 64 disjoint exposed validation pairs. The raw
and protected channels receive the same semantic 1-percent bit-corruption
stream in a registered stress condition. At most three candidates advance.

Fallback nominees after a negative repair gate are diagnostic only. A candidate
that fails repair cannot re-enter the promotable set through a later coverage
or scaling result.

## 3. Coverage and distributed carriers

Advancing renewal mechanisms cross propagation radii 5, 6, 7, and 8 with one
seed, two antipodal seeds, and four quadrant seeds. Two- and four-seed carriers
are tested both by full payload replication and by fixed-total-bit channel
partition. Seed constellations translate together. Registered ablations remove
25 or 50 percent of seeds.

A 16-pair pilot reduces the factorial to at most 12 candidates before a
64-pair generation-8 screen. A durable screen-positive object needs crossover
at least 0.15 with positive lower bound, two positive directions, at least half
of pair effects positive, positive renewal advantage, survival at least 0.90,
translation retention at least 0.70, and corrected-anchor retention at least
0.70. At most one fixed-radius and one distributed candidate advance.

For distributed carriers, encoded zero is a physically present channel rather
than an absent channel. Spatial bands and support are measured from the nearest
member of the translated seed constellation, so the relevant causal region is
the union of its local light cones.

## 4. Scaling and causal geometry

The corrected full anchor and the surviving single- and multi-seed objects are
tested on 16x16, 32x32, and 64x64 tori. Fixed mode retains absolute radius,
writer window, and seed count. Diameter mode uses only nearest-neighbour
propagation and routing but permits time linear in lattice diameter. Density
mode retains fixed local budgets while scaling seed count with area.

Fixed-radius success requires exact zero carrier support outside the light cone
and at least 70-percent retention of within-cone recovery at sizes 32 and 64.
Diameter- or density-scaled success requires at least 70-percent global
retention. Inherited bits, temporary field and writer storage, routing work,
seed density, and shared parameters are reported separately.

The exact-support value is computed from the simulated occupancy mask and is a
registered gate, not a property asserted from the construction alone.

## 5. Qualification, endurance, and final audit

At most two candidates run on 96 exposed pairs, 16 futures per history, to
generation 16 under intact, zero, shuffle, read-off, founder-write-off,
write-off, no-rewrite, ablation, same and opposite rescue, opposite founder,
corruption, transport-off, regeneration-off, consolidation-off, translation,
communication-cut, and founder-clamping conditions.

Qualification requires positive generation-16 recovery of at least 0.10,
positive lower bound, both directions positive, active renewal, registered
loss/rescue/reversal effects, finite causal compliance, translation
robustness, survival at least 0.90, and 70-percent corrected-anchor retention.
Survivors run to generation 64 on 32 exposed endurance pairs; intact recovery
and active-rewrite advantage must retain positive lower bounds.

If and only if a candidate qualifies and endures, at most two winners plus the
full anchor are frozen. Models, order, streams, thresholds, conditions, and
output schema are sealed in `FINAL_AUDIT_DESIGN.json`. The user has authorized
automatic opening of all 62 protected pairs after that seal. The audit uses 24
futures per history through generation 32. No retuning, replacement, or
partial-peeking is permitted.

Final verdicts distinguish durable fixed-radius PH, distributed
local-interaction PH, transient local PH only, whole-lattice coordination only,
and no corrected PH. If no candidate reaches the final gate, reserve
trajectories remain unloaded.
