# Preregistered CA motif-lineage Stage 6: minimal, scalable, evolvable heredity

## Confirmed ancestry and question

Stage 5R ended with the sealed verdict
`ROBUST_REGENERATIVE_LOCAL_64BIT_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`. On 96 untouched
matched founder pairs, both the exact and compact one-site regenerative
carriers passed the complete renewal and localization gates under ordinary
and moderate-joint damage. The compact carrier reached crossover 0.874 at
generation 16 ordinarily and 0.451 under damage; the exact carrier reached
0.955 and 0.743. Removing propagation, regeneration, consolidation, reading,
or rewriting eliminated the effect. Correct rescue restored it and an
opposite-history rescue reversed it.

Stage 6 tests the remaining limitation. The inherited object was local, but
eight propagation steps covered the complete 16x16 torus and the writer used
a 30-step circuit whose endpoint was a whole-lattice census. Stage 6 asks
whether renewal remains possible with incomplete carrier coverage, bounded
writer communication, larger lattices, fewer inherited bits, ecological
competition, and evolutionary search.

No Wagner or Fable implementation source may be read, imported, hashed, or
executed. Claims remain limited to engineered synthetic CA heredity.

## Five mandatory review gates

Stage 6 consists of five separately invoked rounds: `locality`, `scale`,
`compression`, `ecology`, and `audit`. Each round has a four-hour wall limit,
at most 20 workers, atomic checkpoints, a 30-minute orderly-shutdown reserve,
and a decision artifact. No round launches its successor. Reference rounds
after `locality` require the reviewed decision from the preceding round.

The 882 frozen rule-31649 pairs contain 62 still-untouched later-audit pairs.
Their identities and identity digest are already frozen by Stage 5R, but
their trajectories are excluded from every fit, screen, diagnostic, and
evolutionary operation until a separate `audit` invocation with
`--resume --authorize-final-audit`. The other 820 pairs are deterministically
hash-partitioned before outcomes are inspected. Smoke profiles substitute
already-exposed pairs and never touch the reserve.

## Frozen common mechanics

Unless explicitly varied, Stage 6 retains rule 31649, the matched visible
reset, 64 visible sweeps per generation, process noise 0.002, read sweeps
1--32, write sweeps 49--64, observation sweeps 57--64, reader strength 0.25,
the Stage-4 Walsh mode order, the Stage-5R exact-zero quantizer, paired random
streams, phenotype observers, dead-future denominators, pair-level bootstrap,
and all Stage-5R causal thresholds.

The primary object is the confirmed `moment16-ridge` carrier. A boundary seed
contains one occupied site and its quantized values. Inherited values,
occupancy, developmental field values, writer accumulators, routing buffers,
and shared parameters are always reported as different accounting categories.

## Round 6A: bounded locality and endurance

The one-site seed uses synchronous Moore-neighbour consensus copying. Its
registered germination budgets are 2, 4, 5, and 8 hops. On a 16x16 torus only
the eight-hop setting covers the full field; settings of at most five hops are
classified as bounded.

During write sweeps, every site accumulates the selected local Walsh parity
moments. Consolidation uses a directed toroidal shift-and-accumulate circuit.
For span `k`, the circuit performs `k` horizontal one-edge shifts followed by
`k` vertical one-edge shifts and averages the resulting `(k+1)^2` rectangle.
Registered spans are 0, 2, 4, 7, and 15, corresponding to 0, 4, 8, 14, and 30
communication steps. Only span 15 is a whole-lattice census. At most span 7 is
classified as bounded. Impulse and masked-value audits must prove that no
unreachable site affects the endpoint.

The 20 germination/consolidation combinations are screened on 64 exposed
pairs, eight futures per history, and eight generations. The global anchor,
confirmed exact carrier, and confirmed compact carrier are diagnostics. At
most four local candidates advance, including the strongest bounded object
and the confirmed compact anchor. Qualification uses 96 disjoint exposed
pairs, 16 futures per history, and 32 generations. A 32-pair panel follows
the selected bounded object to 64 generations under intact, no-rewrite,
corruption, and communication-cut conditions.

A bounded candidate advances only if it passes the Stage-5R strict renewal
and localization gates at generation 16, has positive generation-32 recovery,
and retains at least 70 percent of the confirmed compact anchor at generation
16. Transport-off, regeneration-off, consolidation-off, communication-cut,
and translation controls are mandatory.

## Round 6B: scale, causal geometry, and rule domain

The best bounded candidate and the compact full-communication anchor are run
on 16x16, 32x32, and 64x64 tori. Larger states are controlled periodic tilings
of the same matched 16x16 founders and reset, so normalized texture targets
remain comparable. All origins, random fields, readers, writers, and
observers must be dimension independent.

The fixed-budget condition keeps the exact 6A hop budgets at all sizes. The
diameter-scaled condition gives germination `extent/2` hops and consolidation
span `extent-1`. Recovery is scored globally and in Chebyshev distance bands
from the seed. Carrier influence outside the registered light cone must be
exactly absent. Fixed-budget global decay is not failure if in-cone recovery
retains 70 percent of the 16x16 value. Diameter-scaled global recovery must
retain 70 percent while communication time grows linearly with diameter.

Without refitting, an exploratory rule-domain panel tests all eighteen
one-bit neighbours of the 18-bit Life-like rule 31649. Rule 31648 is the
registered positive-transfer anchor
and rule 70366 the registered negative-domain anchor. Transfer cannot promote
or rescue a candidate.

## Round 6C: carrier and writer compression

The strongest bounded 6A mechanism is crossed with these frozen payloads:
16x4=64, 12x3=36, 8x3=24, 8x2=16, 4x2=8, and 2x2=4 inherited bits. Mode subsets
follow the archived Walsh order. Signed quantizers retain exact zero. Writer moment dimensions
match payload rank and are fitted only from the frozen label-blind Stage-3R
trace matrix.

Screening uses 64 exposed pairs, eight futures, and eight generations.
Qualification uses 96 disjoint exposed pairs, 16 futures, and 32 generations,
with ordinary and moderate-joint damage and the complete causal ladder. A
separate exposed-pair panel measures erasure, sign corruption, writer-gain
drift, held-out decoder accuracy, and a binary-channel information lower
bound. The output is a Pareto frontier
over inherited bits, temporary writer values, shared parameter bits,
communication steps, and recovery.

Primary success requires at most 32 inherited bits, at most eight writer
moments, strict renewal, and at least 70 percent of the full compact effect.
An 8- or 4-bit generation-32 result is a registered stretch success.

## Round 6D: ecology and evolutionary accessibility

Ecology assays place two seeds in one lattice: same history, opposite history,
intact versus corrupted, unequal precision, and half-channel chimeras. Seed
separation is varied. Spatial domains, wavefronts, takeover, coexistence,
recombination, offspring payloads, and descendant phenotype are recorded.

Evolution has two tracks. `descent` mutates the confirmed compact architecture
toward lower inherited and developmental cost. `de-novo` starts from random
sparse moment writers and does not insert a confirmed solution. The genome
contains a ranked Walsh-prefix mask, precision, germination hops,
consolidation span, sparse quantized writer weights, bias, and reader
strength. Deterministic multi-objective selection rewards held-out recovery and robustness
while penalizing inherited bits, writer values, and communication.

Twelve populations per treatment are run for autocorrelated environments,
IID histories, inheritance disabled, shuffled payload, and rewriting disabled.
Training uses the frozen label-blind Stage-3R trace-reconstruction surrogate;
this proxy is never itself counted as PH. At most four proxy nominees are
followed by 16-generation full-CA causal validation on disjoint exposed pairs.
Evolutionary accessibility requires at
least six of twelve autocorrelated populations to discover held-out positive
renewal, no comparable advantage in IID or inheritance-disabled controls, and
full causal qualification of any final nominee.

## Round 6E: final sealed audit

At most four objects may be frozen: the confirmed 64-bit compact anchor, the
strongest bounded object, the smallest qualified bounded/compressed object,
and an evolved nominee only if it independently qualifies. Missing classes
remain empty; no fallback object is substituted.

All 62 untouched pairs are opened together. The audit uses 48 futures per
history, 16 generations, ordinary and moderate-joint damage, the complete
causal ladder, targeted locality controls, 10,000 pair-cluster bootstrap
resamples, and alpha 0.005 per object. Models, thresholds, cohort identities,
and candidate order are immutable after `FINAL_AUDIT_DESIGN.json` is written.

Verdicts are hierarchical: robust evolvable bounded compressed PH; robust
bounded compressed PH; robust bounded local PH; scale-dependent regenerative
broadcast; Stage-5R-only replication; or failure to replicate.

## Reproducibility and stopping

The implementation must provide smoke, pilot, and reference profiles; exact
design and model hashes; resume-equivalent semantic random streams; atomic
status, queue, checkpoint, report, and lay-summary artifacts; explicit partial
budget states; and tests proving cohort exclusion. A negative or incomplete
round is a result. Thresholds cannot be relaxed and Stage 7 never launches
automatically.
