## Directly specified contract

- ECA width 64, periodic boundary, all 256 rules reduced to the literature's
  88 reflection/conjugation orbits.
- A generation ends after four sweeps and 4W cumulative cell changes, or at 128
  sweeps; a monochrome terminal row is dead.
- Per-sweep flip probability 0.01 and inter-generation copy error 0.015 at the
  atlas operating point.
- Raw composition is the cyclic terminal-row 4-mer census.
- Inheritance is cosine similarity strictly greater than 0.9; equality is a
  break.  Break-by-8 asks whether a break occurs in the first eight boundaries.
- Strict heredity takes the first break, then searches through boundary 32 for
  eight consecutive inherited boundaries whose eight daughters are all-pairs
  coherent above 0.9 and each no more than 0.85 similar to the old anchor.
- The particle observer replaces raw composition by the same 4-mer census on a
  figure mask.  A cell is figure when its 3x3 spatiotemporal code is absent from
  a noiseless rule-domain dictionary (four seeds, 64 burn-in sweeps, 16 collect
  sweeps, 90% mass coverage, at most 64 codes).
- Life uses a 16x16 torus, activity budget 48, four-to-64 sweeps, flip 0.002,
  copy error 0.005, and a generation-averaged live 2x2 census.

## Underspecified choices fixed independently

The result record does not expose the exact seed bit patterns, random stream
construction, whether process flips occur immediately before or after the rule
map, the initial temporal context of the particle observer, or the precise
post-break spectra pooled into an atlas form.  This implementation freezes:

- raw SHA-256-derived seed rows (expected density 0.5, not forced to exactly
  half ones) shared across rules;
- separate SHA-256-derived `random.Random` streams per rule, seed, future, and
  experiment namespace;
- synchronous rule application followed by independent output flips;
- terminal-row death testing after the noisy sweep sequence and before copy;
- one noiseless rule sweep before the raw launch observation, preventing the
  arbitrary hash texture from becoming a universal first-break anchor;
- particle trajectories begin measurement only after the first real
  activity-gated generation; 33 physical generations yield 32 comparable
  figure-mask boundaries, with no fabricated launch-time spacetime window;
- the four noiseless particle-domain seeds span densities 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, and
  0.8, with hash-selected positions; this prevents conserved-density rules
  (especially traffic rule 184) from receiving a one-density dictionary;
- named-Life ensemble forms average the first four observed generation
  compositions (one complete glider phase cycle); fidelity and survival still
  use the full lineage;
- one form per rule/seed: the 50%-mass support of the mean spectrum over all
  live post-first-break daughters across that seed's futures; and
- random-pair atlas nulls drawn from a separately named deterministic stream.

The launch-context choices were fixed after a deliberately underpowered wiring
run showed the two degenerate alternatives directly: zero preparation made the
hash row a nearly universal break, while repeated-row particle context made the
first observer transition incomparable by construction.  That smoke output is
retained under `results/smoke-suite/`; it was not used as confirmatory evidence.
The density-stratified dictionary was fixed after the same audit showed that an
exact-half dictionary collapsed rule 184 to two codes, whereas the retained
contract describes multiple hash seeds and the observer is intended to learn a
rule domain rather than a single conserved-density sector.

These choices are configuration and provenance, not hidden calibration knobs.
They are emitted in every manifest.  Alternate choices can be run explicitly,
but no alternate cell may silently replace the primary result.
