# Novelty Report: Attractors and Committors

## Candidate contributions

The cluster's potentially new contribution is the combination of a branched empirical committor audit with a cross-lineage falsification of the target basin in a GARD reconstruction. It shows that a transition probability can be statistically real and highly reproducible while the completed-run destination used to define that probability is not transferable across independent stochastic lineages.

## Novelty check

Committors and stochastic shooting are established tools. For example, [Machine-guided path sampling to discover mechanisms of molecular self-organization](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-023-00428-z) defines a committor as a fate probability estimated from repeated trajectories and learns reaction coordinates from those outcomes. Machine-learning and transition-path work therefore precede this study.

GARD attractors and compotypes are also established, including [Quasispecies in population of compositional assemblies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25547629/) and [Predicting species emergence in simulated complex pre-biotic networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5813963/). The latter is especially important prior art because it asks whether beta-network organization predicts GARD species without replaying every trajectory.

The targeted search did not find an earlier paper applying independent branch-half reliability, short-propagator confirmation, and reciprocal independent-lineage basin transfer to the same GARD first-entry target. This is evidence of a potentially distinctive analysis, not proof of global priority.

## Defensible novelty statement

> In the reconstructed GARD system, a completed-lineage basin supports a reliable empirical committor and an untouched-confirmed short-propagator coordinate, yet the basin itself fails reciprocal independent-lineage transfer. Transition predictability and destination reproducibility are therefore separable properties.

## Limits

- The result concerns two reconstructed simulator candidates, not physical protocells.
- The original target uses future information and is unsuitable as an operational self-replicator definition.
- The short propagator samples future stochastic updates; it is a computational measurement, not a static biomarker.
- Prior committor and GARD literature prevents claiming novelty for either component in isolation.

## Novelty conclusion

The strongest contribution is a diagnostic lesson for origin-of-life simulation: verify that a destination exists independently before interpreting a successful committor as evidence for emergence into a general attractor. This result directly justifies the process-level pivot that follows.
