# Novelty Report: Foundation

## Candidate contributions

The foundation cluster primarily contributes a reproducibility framework, not a new claim about prebiotic chemistry. Its strongest potentially novel elements are the combination of:

1. a complete executable-ambiguity registry for one GARD/PhiID manuscript;
2. two independent simulator implementations compared at the level of distributions rather than random paths;
3. branch-specific PhiID eligibility with invariance and failure-injection tests; and
4. a hard separation between implementation validity, paper resemblance, prospective prediction, and causal intervention.

## Novelty check

A targeted literature search was performed on 13 August 2026. Earlier work already established the GARD framework and compositional inheritance, including [The molecular roots of compositional inheritance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11735293/), later GARD systems reviews such as [Systems protobiology: origin of life in lipid catalytic networks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30045888/), and the formal PhiID/causal-emergence framework in [Reconciling emergences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08220). Reproducible stochastic simulation, synthetic estimator validation, and sensitivity analysis are also standard scientific practices.

The search did not identify a published audit matching this exact manuscript-specific combination of claim registry, independent GARD engines, label-clock analysis, full atom-level PhiID validation, and fail-closed eligibility. That absence should not be read as proof of priority. The defensible contribution is a unusually complete application of established reproducibility principles to a difficult simulator/source-lineage problem.

## What is not novel

- GARD, compotypes, and compositional heredity are established concepts.
- Distributional validation of stochastic simulators is established methodology.
- PhiID's 16-atom framework is established.
- Synthetic XOR, redundant-copy, and autoregressive fixtures are standard ways to validate information estimators.
- Declaring short time windows statistically underpowered is a validation result, not a new estimator theory.

## Novelty conclusion

`FOUNDATION` should be presented as an enabling methodological contribution: it exposes exactly which implementation choices can and cannot be inferred and prevents later biological claims from inheriting untested numerical assumptions. It should not be marketed as a discovery about the origin of life or as an independent validation of the manuscript.
