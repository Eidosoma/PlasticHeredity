# Novelty Report: Plastic Heredity

## Candidate discovery

The candidate discovery is not “compositional heredity exists.” That is established GARD territory. The more specific result is that, in two reconstructed GARD candidates, inheritance commonly resumes after disruption while exact return to the pre-break composition is nearly absent; hereditary episodes also show modest count-controlled temporal stickiness. This supports a process picture of **plastic renewal among lineage-specific hereditary regimes**.

## Novelty check

Relevant prior work includes:

- [The molecular roots of compositional inheritance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11735293/), which established compositional inheritance in GARD;
- [Quasispecies in population of compositional assemblies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25547629/), which treated compotypes as quasi-stationary compositional states;
- [Transferable Measurements of Heredity in Models of the Origins of Life](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4610668/), which argued for representation-spanning heredity measures rather than model-specific labels;
- [Systems protobiology: origin of life in lipid catalytic networks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30045888/), which describes GARD composomes as homeostatic, attractor-like entities;
- experimental work on [multispecies autocatalytic RNA networks in coacervates](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42004-023-00887-5), demonstrating robust compositional organization in a different physical system.

These sources establish substantial prior art for heredity, compotypes, robustness, and multiple compositional states. They do not appear to report the same branch-based decomposition into break, resumption, old-anchor return, run-order excess, and functional residuals under two GARD reconstruction candidates.

## Incremental contribution

The defensible incremental contribution is methodological and interpretive:

> Break frequency, generic resumption, sustained inheritance, old-composition recovery, and functional restoration were measured as separate stochastic processes. Their separation reveals renewal of hereditary capacity without restoration of the previous molecular identity.

The exact values and conclusions remain simulator-specific. “Plastic heredity” is an interpretive label for this operational pattern, not a newly discovered universal form of life.

## Alternative explanations

- High resumption can follow directly from high marginal inheritance probability.
- The Markov improvement is small and may reflect simple persistence rather than collective memory.
- A three-fission run is a short operational episode.
- The failure of exact return depends on strict H-based old-neighbourhood semantics.
- Functional similarity measures may omit a relevant invariant.

The fixed-count order and cross-fit Markov controls reduce, but do not eliminate, these alternatives.

## Novelty conclusion

The cluster supports a potentially useful conceptual reframing for GARD: heredity may be a renewable process property rather than identity with one master composition. The novelty claim should be limited to this registered decomposition and its cross-candidate evidence, not extended to biological homeostasis, adaptation, or agency.
