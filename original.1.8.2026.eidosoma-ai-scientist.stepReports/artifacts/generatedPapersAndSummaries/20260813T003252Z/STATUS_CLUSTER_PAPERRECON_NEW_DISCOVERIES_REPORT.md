# Novelty Report: Paper Reconstruction

## Candidate contributions

The candidate contribution is a high-resolution forensic adjudication of the 2026 preprint [Causal Architecture Dynamics Prior to Arrival of Self-replicators in a Model of Catalytic Networks Relevant to Origin-of-Life](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28250), not a competing biological discovery. The audit separates:

- visible figure resemblance from exact numerical reconstruction;
- completed-fit association from prospective prediction;
- an executable online scorer from demonstrated causal control;
- label occupancy from the full temporal fingerprint;
- padding-driven all-cell accuracy from valid-cell prediction.

## Novelty check

Searches on 13 August 2026 located the original preprint and its public conceptual lineage, including [Reconciling emergences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08220) and the authors' related 2026 causal-emergence/reinforcement-learning work. No independent publication was found that had already reproduced or refuted this particular GARD paper panel by panel. Because the target paper is itself very recent and its promised author code is unavailable, this search is necessarily provisional.

The specific cross-panel contradiction identified here—roughly 60% Figure 5 majority accuracy versus roughly 88% Table 1 replication probability, alongside unspecified padding/denominator semantics—appears to be a useful forensic observation. Likewise, the finding that a completed-fit Phi branch can resemble Figures 3–4 while its past-only direction reverses is an important warning about temporal leakage. Neither should be called a fundamental new law.

## Relation to prior GARD work

GARD compotypes, compositional inheritance, and attractor-like growth were well established before the target preprint. Relevant prior work includes [The molecular roots of compositional inheritance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11735293/), [Quasispecies in population of compositional assemblies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25547629/), and [Predicting species emergence in simulated complex pre-biotic networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5813963/). These sources mean that reconstructing recurring composition or catalytic-network structure is not by itself novel.

## Defensible novelty statement

A careful statement is:

> This work provides an independent, branch-explicit forensic reconstruction showing that the publicly recoverable pipeline supports partial retrospective resemblance but does not support the tested prospective-prediction or causal-control claims, and that the remaining ambiguity cannot be resolved from public evidence alone.

That statement is bounded to the tested candidates and does not allege error or misconduct. Missing implementation details, a preprint mistake, or a genuinely different private pipeline remain possible.
