# Novelty Report: Process Risk

## Candidate discovery

The strongest candidate discovery is the untouched confirmation of a frozen past-observable coordinate for a process-defined heredity transition:

> Given the present composition, catalytic-network-conditioned state, phase, and recent heredity history, the reconstructed simulator contains transferable information about the probability that heredity will break and a new three-fission hereditary episode will form within twelve future fissions.

The candidate discovery is the conjunction of the operational process, empirical branch probability, graph/state/history representation, and untouched cross-matrix confirmation in two simulator candidates.

## Novelty search and prior art

A targeted search on 13 August 2026 found substantial prior art for every component in isolation:

- GARD compositional inheritance and compotypes: [The molecular roots of compositional inheritance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11735293/) and [Quasispecies in population of compositional assemblies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25547629/).
- Catalytic-network predictors of GARD species: [Predicting species emergence in simulated complex pre-biotic networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5813963/).
- General, transferable heredity metrics: [Transferable Measurements of Heredity in Models of the Origins of Life](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4610668/).
- Repeated shooting and learned committor coordinates for self-organization: [Machine-guided path sampling to discover mechanisms of molecular self-organization](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-023-00428-z).
- Causal-emergence/PhiID formalism: [Reconciling emergences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08220).

No directly matching study was found that defines a GARD event as **break plus new three-fission hereditary episode**, estimates its state-specific F12 probability with independent future branches, and confirms an unchanged full-state graph-plus-history predictor on seed-firewalled matrices in two simulator implementations. This negative search is not proof of priority and may miss unpublished, differently worded, or non-indexed work.

## Relation to the target paper

The target preprint proposes that causal emergence predicts initial self-replication and can be manipulated as a control knob. The confirmed result supports only the broader premise that pre-event organization can be measurable in a catalytic medium. It differs in every important operational respect:

| Target paper framing | Confirmed process-risk result |
|---|---|
| Phi-r/PhiID causal architecture | Full current state + catalytic graph + recent history |
| First appearance of a self-replicator | Future break followed by renewal of a hereditary episode |
| Replicator as a recurring composition | No privileged composition or completed-run target |
| First-quarter prediction | Post-fission state-specific F12 risk |
| Intervention/control claim | No intervention tested |

## Strength of evidence

The untouched L54 evidence is stronger than the adaptive L53 discovery lead because L54 used:

- wholly new matrices and stochastic streams;
- frozen transformations and coefficients;
- independent empirical branch outcomes;
- both candidate simulators;
- overall and within-matrix gates;
- proper-score comparisons against direct history and beta-only controls;
- matrix bootstraps, permutations, and exact regeneration.

It remains weaker than a physical discovery because it is restricted to a reconstructed simulator, one strict-H heredity rule, one run length, one F12 horizon, and five post-fission landmarks.

## Defensible novelty statement

> We identify and independently confirm, within two reconstructed GARD simulator candidates, a target-blind state/network/history coordinate for the stochastic propensity of plastic heredity to break and renew. This coordinate is distinct from PhiID and from entry into a completed-run attractor.

## Immediate falsification priorities

The next separately authorized experiment should treat the coordinate as nonprivileged and test:

- preprocessing, PCA, coefficient, numerical, and target sensitivity;
- alternative H and episode-length definitions fixed before outcomes;
- leakage under suffix replacement;
- calibration and candidate consistency;
- incremental value beyond exact H, direct history, ordinary stability, matrix propensity, and simpler state/process controls;
- whether molecular edits that change the coordinate also change independently measured branch probability.

The last item is an intervention hypothesis, not an achieved result.

## Novelty conclusion

The evidence supports a potentially novel simulator-process result and a new operational framing. It does not justify “paper replicated,” “causal emergence confirmed,” “life detected,” or “causal control.”
