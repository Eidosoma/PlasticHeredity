# Research-Step Cluster Index

## Scope and indexing rule

This index covers the completed evidence for the experiment **Forensic Replication of the Paper** through deterministic S20 closeout. The evidence base contains 93 report-bearing units: 92 execution-level research steps plus the S19 umbrella record. For each unit, the canonical `research_step_full_results.md` was used when available; the step-specific full-results report was used only where the canonical filename was absent. The inventory also contains 439 local visual assets. Fourteen figures selected for scientific relevance and legibility were copied into this report bundle.

The cluster axis is the scientific and epistemic progression of the work, not the chronology of reviewer comments:

1. establish an auditable simulator and information-analysis foundation;
2. reconstruct and adjudicate the paper-facing claims;
3. test whether a transferable attractor destination exists;
4. replace destination labels with process-level plastic heredity;
5. confirm a past-observable coordinate for the probability of a future heredity-regime transition.

No mounted dataset was used. The research inputs were the uploaded manuscript and its extracted figures, validated simulation outputs, pinned public source repositories, and the generated research artifacts.

## Evidence posture

The clusters are cumulative but not interchangeable. `FOUNDATION` validates the mechanics; it does not validate the paper's conclusions. `PAPERRECON` adjudicates resemblance to the manuscript and ends with a constraining verdict. `ATTRACTORS` establishes that stochastic shooting can measure a reliable transition tendency while also showing that the original destination is not transferable. `HEREDITY` identifies a more defensible process—plastic renewal of heredity rather than restoration. `PROCESSRISK` supplies the only untouched positive confirmation: a frozen simulator coordinate that predicts the independently measured probability of that process on new matrices.

The confirmed process result does not retroactively convert any Phi/PhiID, first-replicator, or intervention claim into support.
