## Outcome and stop boundary

The run advanced through the preregistration, implementation lock, pinned-source verification, prior-artifact immutability audit, and source-metric identity gate. It then launched all 24 preregistered confirmation simulations without computing an emergence outcome.

Twenty-three lineages completed exactly 100 fissions. Confirmation lineage `E01-S12D-C02` instead reached mass zero during growth generation 5:

- 1,192 molecular events were recorded.
- Four post-fission states were completed.
- The terminal event was a loss event changing mass from 1 to 0.
- The final observation index was 1,196, with 1,197 total observation rows.
- No fifth post-fission state existed.

The locked label adapter supplied the preregistered 100 generation identifiers to the four available post-fission states, causing `LabelContractError: observation_ids length must equal the state row count.` This was the first fatal exception surfaced to the orchestrator. A read-only post-failure audit established that the underlying condition was lineage extinction, not source-metric disagreement.

The frozen design required exactly 24 complete 100-fission units and required all matrices, initial states, event streams, fission streams, trajectories, labels, and preprocessing outputs to be hash-frozen before any emergence outcome could be accessed. That gate failed. Consequently:

- consolidated confirmation artifacts were not promoted;
- the seed/data firewall was not released;
- the existing S12C diagnostic table was not opened by the scientific run;
- no completed-fit emergence value was computed;
- no past-only prefix emergence value was computed;
- no association, spike, metric-identity, or future-dependence analysis was run;
- no S12D figure was generated.

### Confirmation generation

The unchanged S12 historical GARD specification was used with the separately frozen 256-bit S12D root and domain-separated streams. Exactly 24 matrix tasks, indexed 0–23, were launched with six workers. The intended trajectory length was 100 fissions per matrix. The common substrate was additive-`0.5` closure, full CLR, and removal of original component 100. Historical `H>0.9` and past-only cosine were the only authorized labels.

Generation, labeling, preprocessing, and hash promotion were performed before any source-emergence call. This ordering ensured that an incomplete confirmation set could not leak into the scientific analysis.

None of these analyses was reached.
