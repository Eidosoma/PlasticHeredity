# Reviewer direction carried into L51

The operative framing is plastic heredity and stochastic switching between hereditary and nonhereditary regimes. Break probability, resumption conditional on a break and their joint probability remain distinct. L51 compares IID, first-order Markov, duration-dependent semi-Markov/renewal and matrix-specific propensity models, separates between-matrix from within-matrix variation, and does not require a monotonic ramp. PhiID and functional-memory searches remain out of scope because prior loops found no incremental heldout value.
