- **Measurement/proxy boundaries:**

  - Self-replicator labels are operational proxies for compositional reproduction and depend on recurrence, clustering, persistence, and threshold choices.
  - \(\widehat{\Phi}^r\) is an estimator-dependent information-dynamic quantity, not a direct measurement of biological agency, life, cognition, or physical causal power.
  - Attractor distance, committors, causal closure, effective information, stress variables, and causal-boundary scores are computational constructs evaluated within the simulated model.
  - The voltage layer in E07 is a stylized coordination analogue, not a validated model of protocell electrophysiology.
  - Any relationship to real origin-of-life chemistry remains hypothetical until tested experimentally.
- **Out-of-scope claims:** Experimental validation in prebiotic chemistry; equivalence of GARD composomes to living organisms; consciousness or subjective experience; proof of Platonic forms; real membrane electrophysiology; biomedical treatment claims; universal applicability across origin-of-life scenarios; or use of compute resources allocated to unrelated projects.

## Strategic Principle

- **Decomposition strategy:** Execute a serial seven-Experiment program. First reconstruct the paper, then adjudicate alternative explanations, discover robust regimes, locate the operative individual, test compositional memory, test operational minimal agency, and finally test higher-level collective organization.
- **Experiment scope summary:**
  1. E01 reconstructs and audits the paper.
  2. E02 tests leakage, preprocessing, labels, estimators, attractor geometry, and intervention controls.
  3. E03 explores validated variables across a larger GARD morphospace.
  4. E04 tests molecule-, module-, assembly-, and coalition-level individuality.
  5. E05 tests attractor commitment, regenerative return, and compositional memory.
  6. E06 tests operational minimal competency using restricted embedded controllers.
  7. E07 tests collective pattern maintenance, distributed memory, and group heredity.
- **Separate-workspace rationale:** Each Experiment has a distinct scientific objective, compute profile, validation gate, and artifact bundle. Later Experiments must consume frozen outputs and adjudications from earlier work rather than mutate the earlier evidence base.
- **Research-step planning boundary:** The 112 research steps are bounded slices within the seven planned Experiments. Additional scientific branches are not additional steps or Experiments unless a later group-level decision explicitly approves them.
- **Dataset boundary:** No public observational dataset Experiment is required. The principal data are generated by simulation. Source-code snapshots and the uploaded paper are research inputs, not public dataset payloads.

Experiment design principle:

> Start from forensic reconstruction, make every important ambiguity explicit, demand prospective and paired causal evidence, preserve negative findings, and permit the program to replace \(\Phi^r\) when a simpler or more robust reaction coordinate performs better.

Terminology:

- **planned Experiment:** A group-plan unit with a stable Experiment number, one scientific objective, and one workspace-agent run.
- **research step:** A bounded unit of work performed inside one Experiment workspace.
- **follow-up Experiment:** A separately approved future scope that must not be silently folded into the present seven-Experiment program.
- **causal-architecture variable:** \(\Phi^r\) or a replacement variable that survives the E02 adjudication.
- **replicator:** An operationally defined recurrent compositional state, not a claim that the assembly is biologically alive.

## Frozen Baseline Model

### GARD state

Let

\[
\mathbf n(t)=\left(n_1(t),\ldots,n_{N_g}(t)\right),\qquad
N(t)=\sum_{i=1}^{N_g}n_i(t),\qquad
x_i(t)=\frac{n_i(t)}{N(t)}.
\]

The baseline catalytic matrix is

\[
\beta_{ij}=\exp\left(A+\sigma\epsilon_{ij}\right),
\qquad
\epsilon_{ij}\sim\mathcal N(0,1),
\]

with

\[
N_g=100,\quad n_{\min}=40,\quad n_{\max}=80,\quad
A=-4,\quad \sigma=4,\quad n_{\mathrm{gen}}=100,\quad
\mathrm{max\ steps}=1000.
\]

The historical public implementation motivates

\[
b_i(\mathbf n)=1+\frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N_g}\beta_{ij}n_j,
\]

\[
a_i^+(\mathbf n)=k_f\rho_iN\,b_i(\mathbf n),
\qquad
a_i^-(\mathbf n)=k_bn_i\,b_i(\mathbf n).
\]

Historical public defaults \(k_f=10^{-2}\) and \(k_b=10^{-4}\) are reference settings, not assumed author settings. The values of \(k_f\), \(k_b\), \(\rho_i\), and the exact stochastic update semantics remain explicit E01 ambiguities.

For a Gillespie-compatible interpretation,

\[
a_0=\sum_i(a_i^++a_i^-),\qquad
P(\mu=k)=\frac{a_k}{a_0},\qquad
\Delta t\sim\operatorname{Exp}(a_0).
\]

The paper describes stochastic Poisson updates with a maximum step count. E01 must distinguish historical-code behavior, paper-described behavior, and a modern Gillespie interpretation rather than conflating them.

Growth stops at \(N=n_{\max}\) or `max_steps`. Fission is reconstructed as

\[
n_{i,g+1}\sim
\operatorname{Binomial}
\left(n_{i,g}^{\mathrm{pre-fission}},0.5\right),
\]

after which one daughter is followed.

### Zero-safe compositional preprocessing

For pseudocount \(\delta\),

\[
x_i^{(\delta)}
=
\frac{n_i+\delta}{N+N_g\delta},
\]

\[
z_i=
\log x_i^{(\delta)}
-\frac{1}{N_g}\sum_{j=1}^{N_g}\log x_j^{(\delta)}.
\]

The frozen pseudocount grid is

\[
\delta\in
\left\{
10^{-6},10^{-4},10^{-2},0.1,0.5,1
\right\}.
\]

The paper-like dropped-final-component CLR representation must be compared with all dropped-component choices and with the ILR representation

\[
\mathbf y=\mathbf V^\top\log\mathbf x^{(\delta)},
\]

where \(\mathbf V\) is an orthonormal basis of the simplex subspace. Multiplicative zero replacement, raw proportions, Hellinger coordinates, and principal log-ratio coordinates appear as adversarial alternatives.

### Replicator labels

Historical GARD similarity is

\[
H(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)=
\frac{\mathbf x^\top\mathbf y}
{\lVert\mathbf x\rVert_2\lVert\mathbf y\rVert_2}.
\]

Maintain at least:

\[
Y_g^H=
\mathbb 1\{\text{historical non-drift or compotype rule}\},
\]

\[
Y_g^E=
\mathbb 1\{\text{persistent Euclidean cluster}\},
\]

\[
Y_g^A=
\mathbb 1\{\text{persistent Aitchison-space cluster}\},
\]

and

\[
R_g=\max_{h&lt;g}H(\mathbf x_g,\mathbf x_h).
\]

Parent-daughter fidelity, basin-return probability, and continuous recurrence are required alternatives in E02.

### Causal-emergence reconstruction

For a past-only window

\[
W_t=\{\mathbf Z_{t-w+1},\ldots,\mathbf Z_t\}
\]

and lag \(\tau\), reconstruct the supplied operational cut statistic and the precise \(\Phi\)ID atom used by the frozen reference implementation. The exact redundancy function, minimum-information-partition normalization, partition search, numerical estimator, and information atom identified as \(\Phi^r\) must be written into every run manifest.

All prospective analyses must obey

\[
\widehat{\Phi}^r(t)
\text{ uses only observations indexed }s\le t.
\]

Whole-trajectory estimates are permitted only as explicitly non-prospective descriptive reconstructions.

Primary windows and lags are

\[
w\in\{32,64,128,256\},
\qquad
\tau\in\{1,2,4,8\}.
\]

### Primary outcomes

For replicator state \(Y_t\),

\[
O=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}Y_t,
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{total}}=\sum_{t=1}^{T}Y_t,
\]

\[
L_{\max}=\max_k L_k,
\qquad
\overline L=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}L_k,
\qquad
\tau_1=\min\{t:Y_t=1\}.
\]

Retain three consistency candidates until E01 determines which reproduces Table 1:

\[
C_Y=\operatorname{corr}(Y_t,Y_{t+1}),
\]

\[
C_H=\frac{1}{T-1}\sum_tH(\mathbf x_t,\mathbf x_{t+1}),
\]

\[
C_Z=
\operatorname{corr}
\left(
\operatorname{vec}\mathbf Z_{1:T-1},
\operatorname{vec}\mathbf Z_{2:T}
\right).
\]

### Intervention operator

At post-fission state \(\mathbf n_g\),

\[
\mathcal A(\mathbf n_g)=
\{0\}
\cup\{+\mathbf e_i:i=1,\ldots,N_g\}
\cup\{-\mathbf e_i:n_i&gt;0\}.
\]

The maximizing action is

\[
a_g^{\max}
=
\arg\max_{a\in\mathcal A}
\widehat{\Phi}^r
\left(
W_g\oplus T_a(\mathbf n_g)
\right),
\]

with an analogous minimizing action.

Evaluate three interpretations:

1. Append-only score.
2. One-step expected score over stochastic next events.
3. Generation-rollout score over \(K\) complete growth-fission rollouts.

The append-only score is the likely paper reconstruction; generation-rollout scoring is the strongest prospective interpretation.

## Guardrails

- **Scientific guardrails:**
  - Do not combine distinct E01 claims into one replication verdict.
  - Distinguish descriptive whole-trajectory analyses from prospective past-only analyses.
  - Preserve all preregistered valid specifications, including negative and null results.
  - Do not change labels, thresholds, estimators, or primary endpoints retrospectively without a new versioned specification.
  - Do not infer causal control from prediction alone.
  - Require paired counterfactual or randomized intervention evidence for intervention claims.
- **Safety guardrails:** The work is computational and does not include chemical synthesis, wet-lab procedures, biological release, or actionable biomedical intervention.
- **Cost guardrails:**
  - Run a minimal reproducible run before every scale-up.
  - Use no more than 8 CPU cores and one GPU-heavy job at a time.
  - Pause when an Experiment exceeds its compute ceiling before half of planned conditions complete.
  - Benchmark storage and runtime before broad sweeps.
- **Data guardrails:**
  - Preserve integer states and event logs wherever feasible.
  - Never overwrite raw trajectories.
  - Derived data must include source trajectory IDs, specification IDs, and seed identities.
  - More than 5% non-regenerable trajectories triggers a pause.
- **Claim-boundary guardrails:**
  - Simulation evidence does not establish behavior in real chemistry.
  - Operational agency and intelligence classifications do not imply consciousness.
  - Attractor equivalence classes are mathematical relational classes, not metaphysical proof.
  - Collective benefit must exceed isolated and passive-coupling controls.
- **Negative claims:** A failure to detect an effect is scoped to tested estimators, regimes, power, and intervention budgets. It is not proof that no causal architecture, memory, agency, or collective organization is possible.

## Robustness And Falsification

- **Stress tests:** Parameter sweeps, zero-handling choices, coordinate systems, windows, lags, partition stability, labels, estimator families, catalytic-matrix perturbations, environmental changes, and fission variants.
- **Controls:** No action, random action, sign-matched action, displacement-matched action, attractor-directed action, shuffled-\(\Phi\) action, passive diffusion, proportional control, open-loop control, and oracle controls where appropriate.
- **Holdouts:** Untouched catalytic matrices in E03; new matrices in E04–E07; structurally novel perturbations in E06; new collective topologies or damage conditions in E07.
- **Ablations:** Remove \(\Phi^r\), recurrence, attractor distance, memory, sensors, causal-boundary variables, catalytic cores, coupling channels, voltage state, or selected controller components.
- **Null models:** Block permutation, circular shifts, phase-randomized surrogates, duration-preserving label shuffles, shuffled catalytic matrices, randomized molecular identities, iid sparse compositions, matched random partitions, and degree- or weight-preserving network nulls.
- **Failure criteria:**
  - Stop claiming first-arrival prediction if the effect vanishes after excluding runs with replication inside the input interval.
  - Stop claiming an independent control knob if max-\(\Phi^r\) fails to outperform displacement-matched and attractor-directed controls.
  - Stop claiming hidden memory if exactly state-matched clones have indistinguishable future distributions.
  - Stop claiming higher-level collective intelligence if collective benefit is fully explained by independent local recovery.
  - Archive the original \(\Phi^r\)-centered branch after E02 if E01 fails all central directions, past-only estimates have no prospective value, intervention effects do not beat matched controls, and no coherent regime contains a robust alternative signal.

## Program-Level Gates And Stopping Rules

### Continue

Continue the full program when E01 produces at least a partial replication and E02 identifies a robust signal or useful reaction coordinate.

### Redirect

Redirect from \(\Phi^r\) to a validated alternative when the original estimator is unstable but the underlying transition remains reproducible.

### Pause

Pause scale-up when:

- CPU or GPU use exceeds the Experiment ceiling before half the planned conditions finish.
- More than 5% of trajectories cannot be regenerated from seed manifests.
- CPU and GPU information-dynamic implementations disagree beyond declared precision.
- A label or metric changes retrospectively without a new specification version.
- Numerical instability, singular covariance, invalid entropy estimates, or storage corruption is unresolved.

### Program kill criterion

Archive the original \(\Phi^r\)-centered program after E02 when all of the following hold:

1. E01 fails to reproduce association, prediction, and intervention direction.
2. Corrected past-only estimates produce no prospective value.
3. Intervention effects do not exceed random or matched controls.
4. No coherent parameter region exhibits a robust alternative causal-architecture signal.

The GARD engines, validation suite, and attractor-analysis infrastructure remain reusable under this outcome.
