# Strict-eight rulebook probe — lay summary

This post-hoc follow-up asked whether each catalytic matrix acts like a
landscape that favors particular molecular compositions. We computed a
preferred form directly from the frozen simulator equations, tested whether
one implementation's independent futures revealed the other implementation's
holding propensity, and then moved molecules toward or away from the computed
form before generating 896,000 fresh futures.

The predictive result was strong. Matrix-derived features and
opposite-candidate holding measurements improved held-out prediction of both
eight-way coherence and complete strict-eight occurrence beyond an already
rich history, concentration, and current-state baseline. This shows that the
matrix carries transferable information, although the opposite-candidate
score requires extra futures and is not available from one untouched state.

The causal result was narrower. Moving four molecules toward rather than away
from the preferred form raised the chance of eight mutually coherent
descendants from about 5.2% to 6.0%, an absolute increase of about 0.82
percentage points. All four candidate-by-replicate estimates pointed the same
way with positive confidence intervals. Most of the contrast came from moving
away damaging coherence; moving toward produced a smaller improvement over
doing nothing.

The edit did not clearly change the probability of an initial break, eight
ordinary parent-to-daughter inheritance successes, or the complete strict-
eight event. Its imposed alignment also faded over time and was unresolved by
fission 32.

The frozen global causal gate remained formally negative. The complete
four-molecule edit was feasible in 89.7% of candidate-03 states, just below the
90% requirement, so only two of four cells received a formal pass even though
all four coherence estimates and intervals were positive.

The best-supported interpretation is therefore that matrix-conditioned
alignment contributes to the mutual-coherence part of strict eight. It does
not establish that a rulebook causes the complete event, creates a permanent
attractor, or supplies full two-daughter reproductive fidelity.
