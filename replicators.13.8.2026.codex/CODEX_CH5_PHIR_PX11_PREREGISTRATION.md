# Codex Chapter 5 PX11: causal information redistribution and sensor program

Status: prospective staged registration source. No PX11 scientific matrix,
restored state, intervention future, atom reading, or sensor result may be
generated before validation and registration are sealed.

## Purpose and immutable predecessors

PX11 follows three prespecified leads from PX10 without reopening a formula
tournament:

1. test whether causally increased post-break hereditary renewal produces a
   short-lived redistribution among calibrated PhiID atoms;
2. test a one-parameter, shared molecular-dose channel in place of PX10's
   noisy state-by-arm probability table; and
3. test whether the frozen PX10 temporal score remains a useful ensemble
   gauge under reduced support and degraded observations.

PX1--PX10 remain immutable. In particular, PX11 cannot overwrite the null
public nine-atom result, rename a new summary as Phi-r, or turn a correlate
into a cause. The 24-matrix stage is developmental. Its results must be
reported and reviewed before any separately registered 48-matrix
confirmation. Confirmation never starts automatically.

## Shared post-break experiment

The pilot uses 24 entirely fresh catalytic matrices and the optional
confirmation uses another 48. Both candidates and natural landmarks 20, 35,
50, 65, and 80 are retained. From each untreated landmark, follow at most 60
fissions to the first strict inheritance break (`H <= 0.9`) and save the exact
selected daughter. Every arm starts from that identical already-broken state.

Launch 128 F8 futures per arm, with A = branches 0--63 and B = 64--127.
The arm is absent from the future seed. Once states diverge, arms share common
random streams rather than identical realized futures. No future is retried
and no adverse or extinct matrix is replaced. Every future is replayed.

The fixed arm panel is:

- frozen renewal-student molecular doses `Q00`, `Q20`, `Q40`, `Q60`, `Q80`,
  and `Q100`;
- `RULE_STABILIZE` and `RULE_DESTABILIZE`, using Codex's frozen outgoing
  influence `x @ beta == beta.T @ x`;
- occupied-web `TIGHTEN` (multiply by 1.5) and `LOOSEN` (divide by 1.5);
- `RANDOM_SWAP`, exact-norm throughput-neutral occupied-block `BLOCK_RANDOM`,
  and `NOOP` controls.

The common endpoint is three consecutive inherited fissions within F8 from
the already-broken state. Expected renewal directions are `Q100 > Q00`,
`RULE_STABILIZE > RULE_DESTABILIZE`, and `TIGHTEN > LOOSEN`.

## PX11-A: information redistribution

Use the frozen PX10 local-MMI PhiID implementation, unedited-beta Fiedler
partition, explicit generational pairs, and lags 1, 2, 4, and 8. Pool pairs
over eligible landmarks within a catalytic matrix, never across matrices.
For each real reading subtract the mean of eight depth-preserving future
derangements.

Report all 16 atoms. The five registered label-symmetric groups are:

- downward routing: `s_to_u0 + s_to_u1`;
- upward integration: `u0_to_s + u1_to_s`;
- redundant persistence: `r_to_r`;
- synergy persistence: `s_to_s`;
- cross-part transfer: `u0_to_u1 + u1_to_u0`.

The explicitly new empirical Information Redistribution Index is

    IRI = downward routing + upward integration
          - redundant persistence - synergy persistence
          - cross-part transfer.

IRI is a fingerprint summary, not Phi-r, integrated information, causal
emergence, or a consciousness quantity. The primary response is high-renewal
minus low-renewal mean IRI over lags 1--4. Early-to-late attenuation is mean
lags 1--2 minus mean lags 4 and 8. The exact public nine-atom sum remains a
non-winning negative control.

Each causal family is classified independently. A common causal
redistribution architecture requires positive IRI with a positive 95%
whole-matrix bootstrap lower bound and Holm-adjusted sign-randomization
`p < 0.05` in all three families, both candidates, and both halves. Partial
classifications are retained without pooling failed cells.

## PX11-B: shared molecular-dose channel

The six quantile arms use state-centred frozen predicted shifts. For each
candidate and source half, estimate one signed shared logistic dose
coefficient in the pilot. The offset for each state is the Jeffreys-smoothed
source-half NOOP probability. Coefficients and within-candidate shift scales
are frozen before confirmation.

Confirmation estimates only the same-state NOOP offset from the source half,
then scores the untouched opposite half. A channel passes only with positive
held-out log-loss gain, positive 95% matrix-bootstrap lower bound, and
Holm-adjusted `p < 0.05` in A-to-B and B-to-A for both candidates. Sixteen
arm-label derangements and the `RANDOM_SWAP`/`NOOP` channel must be equivalent
to zero within +/-0.0005 bits per branch.

## PX11-C: degraded-observation ensemble sensor

The frozen score is

    T_temporal = T_beta(real generational transitions)
                 - mean T_beta(eight depth-preserving derangements),

where `T_beta = I(X;X') - I(A;A') - I(B;B')` uses the unedited-beta partition.
It is an ensemble temporal gauge, not a single-assembly sensor.

The pilot scores a fixed nested panel: full observations with 16, 32, or 64
branches per half; 75%, 50%, or 25% fixed coordinate masks at support 64;
50% or 25% molecule-count thinning at support 64; fission-depth strides 2 or
4 at support 64; and fixed compact and minimal combined profiles. Masks and
thinning streams are arm-free and outcome-independent. Invalid empty or
rank-deficient readings remain missing and are never repaired from outcomes.

The cheapest profile is selected in this frozen order: minimal combined,
compact combined, then single-degradation profiles from strongest to weakest,
then full 16, 32, and 64. Eligibility requires positive A/B reliability,
positive `Q100-Q00` response, positive source-score/opposite-half renewal
association in both candidates, and at least 75% of the full-support response.
The selected profile is frozen before confirmation.

After fresh results are sealed, retained CR6 regimes may be deterministically
replayed as a retrospective transport stress test. They cannot count as fresh
confirmation or select another sensor.

## Pilot advancement, inference, and operation

The catalytic matrix is the inference unit. Use 4,096 whole-matrix bootstrap
draws, 4,096 paired whole-matrix sign randomizations, and Holm correction
within each named family. Candidate and branch-half cells are never pooled to
rescue disagreement. Probability equivalence uses a complete 90% interval
inside +/-0.025.

A causal knob advances from the pilot only if its renewal contrast passes all
four candidate/half cells, its IRI point estimate has the registered sign in
all four cells, the sign survives every leave-one-matrix-out estimate, at
least 20 eligible matrices per candidate remain, and replay is exact. The
dose channel advances only if both candidate coefficients and all four
developmental held-out gains are positive. A sensor profile advances only by
the frozen eligibility and cost order above.

All scientific execution is detached, limited to eight workers, checkpointed
per catalytic matrix, and bounded to 30 process CPU-hours across the pilot,
confirmation, and optional transport stage. Large traces, arrays, logs, and
checkpoints remain ignored by Git. The 24-matrix report is a mandatory manual
stop; no authorization artifact is created automatically.

## Claim boundaries

PX11 may support a Codex-specific transient information redistribution, a
low-dimensional molecular action channel, or a robust temporal ensemble
gauge. It cannot rescue the public Phi-r formulation, show that Phi-r causes
plastic heredity, support consciousness or agency, establish biological
memory or life, validate a universal origin-of-life mechanism, establish real
prebiotic chemistry, or support metaphysical claims about Platonic space or
the Ruliad.
