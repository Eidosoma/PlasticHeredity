## Purpose and immutable predecessors

PX8 is the final, narrow follow-up to PX7. PX7 tested eighteen formulations on
two plastic-heredity axes. None passed its complete family-wise gate. The exact
public molecular/self/revised nine-atom Phi-r had the wrong direction for
resistance and no coherent resilience response. Those findings are immutable.

PX7 nevertheless produced one explicitly named development lead:
`generational__beta__typeset` rose under resilience-promoting edits in all four
candidate-by-source-half cells and forecast opposite-half renewal most clearly
in candidate 03. It failed the complete gate because reliability and forecast
did not generalize across every cell and because its causal-response sign was
opposite at 16-branch versus 32-branch source support.

PX8 asks one question:

> With substantially greater estimator support and a completely fresh cohort,
> does the fixed generational, beta-partitioned, unnormalized whole-minus-parts
> statistic prospectively gauge recovery of heredity after an identical
> naturally occurring break?

No other formulation can win. Strict-eight, resistance, consciousness, agency,
life, and metaphysical interpretations are outside scope.

## Cohort and causal experiment

- 48 entirely fresh catalytic matrices, shared across candidates 02 and 03.
- Natural landmarks 20, 35, 50, 65, and 80.
- From each landmark, follow the untreated lineage for at most 60 fissions to
  its first strict inheritance break (`H <= 0.9`).
- Save the exact selected daughter immediately after that break and restore the
  identical state across all arms.
- Use the already frozen candidate-separated CR5 renewal students without
  refit, recalibration, threshold change, or model selection.
- Arms: `RENEWAL_UP`, `RENEWAL_DOWN`, `RANDOM`, and `NOOP`.
- Launch 256 F8 futures per arm and state under common random streams. Arm is
  absent from the future seed key.
- Branch half A is 0--127 and B is 128--255.
- A positive endpoint is three consecutive inherited fissions within F8.
  Certification before later extinction remains positive; extinction before
  certification is negative.
- At least 40 distinct eligible matrices per candidate are required. No failed,
  extinct, or adverse matrix may be replaced.
- Every future is generated twice and must replay exactly.

The catalytic matrix is always the inference unit. States, landmarks, arms,
halves, and repeated observations from one matrix remain together.

## Frozen target information reading

The only eligible reading is `generational__beta__typeset`:

1. Use explicit parent-to-selected-daughter transition pairs. Never join branch
   boundaries or create a transition between independent futures.
2. Add 0.5 to counts, close compositions, apply CLR, and drop the final CLR
   coordinate.
3. Remove only dimensions with effectively zero variance and standardize the
   remaining coordinates over the joined explicit past/future pair block,
   exactly as in the frozen PX7 scorer.
4. Partition molecular coordinates with the deterministic Fiedler split of the
   symmetrized `log1p(beta)` graph. The partition is fixed by beta and does not
   use outcomes or future observations.
5. Compute the unnormalized full-dimensional statistic

       I(X_t; X_t+1) - I(A_t; A_t+1) - I(B_t; B_t+1).

The implementation is the exact PX7 scorer, whose source hash is included in
the PX8 seal. No normalization, atom reweighting, sign change, calibration,
partition search, regularization search, or candidate-specific switch is
allowed.

The public `molecular__self__revised` nine-atom score is retained as a named
negative control at maximum support. It has no pass path and cannot replace the
eligible reading.

## Estimator-support ladder

Each 128-branch source half is scored at fixed nested prefixes of 16, 32, 64,
and 128 branches. With an F8 horizon these ordinarily provide up to 128, 256,
512, and 1,024 explicit generational transition pairs. Extinction is retained,
so achieved transition counts are reported rather than assumed.

The 128-branch reading is primary. Support convergence additionally requires:

1. finite readings in at least 95% of registered arm/state/half cells at both
   64 and 128 branches;
2. positive `RENEWAL_UP - RENEWAL_DOWN` information effects in both candidates
   and both source halves at both 64 and 128 branches;
3. no 64-to-128 sign reversal in any of those four cells; and
4. the median absolute 64-to-128 effect change across the four cells is smaller
   than the median absolute 32-to-64 change.

The 16- and 32-branch results remain fully visible even if they disagree. No
support point may be discarded.

## Primary gauge tests

At 128-branch source support:

1. **Reliability:** A-half and B-half readings must have positive within-matrix
   Spearman association separately in both candidates.
2. **Opposite-half forecast:** the source-half reading must positively rank the
   independently realized renewal probability in the opposite half for A-to-B
   and B-to-A, separately in both candidates.
3. **Causal response:** `RENEWAL_UP - RENEWAL_DOWN` must raise the information
   reading separately in both candidates and both source halves.

Every registered cell must have a positive point estimate, positive 95%
whole-matrix bootstrap lower bound, and Holm-adjusted one-sided whole-matrix
sign-randomization `p < 0.05`. Reliability, forecast, and response are separate
complete Holm families. Use 4,096 bootstrap draws and 4,096 sign draws.

The causal renewal manipulation itself must pass in both candidates and both
target halves: positive `RENEWAL_UP - RENEWAL_DOWN`, positive 95% lower bound,
Holm-adjusted `p < 0.05`, and `RANDOM - NOOP` TOST equivalence within +/-0.025.

`specialized_resilience_gauge_confirmed` is true only if eligibility, renewal
manipulation validity, support convergence, all primary gauge tests, exact
replay, and artifact readback pass. Candidate pooling cannot rescue a failure.
