# Codex Chapter 5 PX9: high-support gauge-identity pilot

Status: prospective registration source. No PX9 scientific catalytic matrix,
restored state, intervention future, information reading, or outcome may be
generated until the source, tests, validation, frozen models, protocol, and
seed registry are sealed.

## Purpose and immutable predecessors

PX8 prospectively confirmed one narrowly specified high-support extension:
the generational, beta-Fiedler-partitioned, unnormalized whole-minus-within
statistic tracked post-break hereditary renewal. The exact public molecular,
self-partitioned, revised nine-atom Phi-r remained null or negative. PX8 also
found no incremental log-loss gain from its raw extension beyond the frozen
renewal predictor. Every earlier result remains immutable.

PX9 does not search for another favorable Phi-r definition. It asks what the
confirmed PX8 extension is measuring:

1. Does its response require the genuine parent-to-daughter pairing?
2. Does the beta-derived partition carry special information, or do arbitrary
   size-matched molecule partitions behave similarly?
3. Does the reading add forecast information beyond ordinary observed
   plastic-heredity summaries?
4. Does it vary gradually across prospectively selected molecular-edit doses?

The exact public revised Phi-r remains a non-winning negative control. No
result may overwrite PX8 or rescue the public score.

## Pilot cohort and manual continuation barrier

- 24 entirely fresh catalytic matrices shared across candidates 02 and 03.
- Natural landmarks 20, 35, 50, 65, and 80.
- From each landmark, follow the untreated lineage for at most 60 fissions to
  the first strict inheritance break (`H <= 0.9`).
- Save the exact selected daughter immediately after that break and restore it
  across every intervention arm.
- At least 20 distinct eligible matrices per candidate are required. Failed,
  extinct, or adverse matrices are never replaced.
- Launch 256 F8 futures per arm and state under common random streams.
- Half A is branches 0--127; half B is branches 128--255.
- A positive renewal endpoint is three consecutive inherited fissions within
  F8. Certification before later extinction remains positive; extinction
  before certification is negative.
- Every future is regenerated in a complete deterministic replay.

This is a prospective 24-matrix mechanistic pilot. It cannot automatically
launch, authorize, or count as a 48-matrix confirmation. Any larger campaign
requires human review, a new registration, and entirely fresh matrices.

## Frozen edit-dose arms

Use the frozen candidate-separated CR5 renewal students, transformations,
coefficients, and prediction mapping without refit or recalibration. Score
every legal mass-preserving one-molecule substitution exhaustively.

Select empirical order statistics at fixed predicted-probability quantiles:

    Q00, Q20, Q40, Q60, Q80, Q100

with quantiles 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, and 1. Deterministic ties use the
lexicographically smallest `(remove_type, add_type)` edit at the selected
probability. Also include:

- `RANDOM`: one uniformly sampled legal edit from a separate action stream;
- `NOOP`: the unchanged broken state.

The arm identity is absent from the future seed. Random edit selection never
consumes the future stream.

## Frozen primary extension

For explicit parent-to-selected-daughter generational transition pairs:

1. add 0.5 to counts, close compositions, apply CLR, and drop the final CLR
   coordinate;
2. remove only effectively invariant coordinates and standardize the joined
   past/future block exactly as in PX7 and PX8;
3. split molecular coordinates with the deterministic Fiedler partition of
   the symmetrized `log1p(beta)` graph; and
4. compute

       T_beta = I(X_t; X_t+1)
                - I(A_t; A_t+1)
                - I(B_t; B_t+1).

The beta partition is fixed by the unedited catalytic matrix and is shared by
all molecular-edit arms. No sign change, normalization, atom reweighting,
regularization search, outcome-based partition, or candidate-specific switch
is permitted. Source supports of 64 and 128 branches per half are retained;
128 is primary.

## Temporal-pairing null

At 128-branch support, construct eight deterministic future derangements.
Within each state, arm, source half, and fission depth, cyclically reassign
future daughters among the branches that reached that depth. This preserves:

- the exact past and future marginal samples;
- fission depth;
- arm, state, candidate, and half;
- extinction and transition-count geometry;

while removing the true parent-to-daughter pairing. No reassigned transition
may retain its original daughter when at least two branches are available.

Define the primary pairing-corrected reading:

    T_temporal = T_beta(real pairs)
                 - mean_k T_beta(derangement k).

Its `Q100 - Q00` contrast is a pairing-by-dose interaction. A positive raw
contrast without a positive corrected contrast is not evidence for a temporal
information response.

## Partition nulls

At 128-branch support, score the real paired transitions under:

- eight deterministic random species partitions with exactly the same global
  part sizes as the beta partition; and
- three beta-Fiedler partitions obtained from fixed, outcome-independent
  cross-matrix beta derangements.

Define:

    T_topology = T_beta
                 - median_r T_size_matched_random_partition_r.

Size matching makes the main finite-dimensional estimator bias common to the
comparison. Cross-matrix partitions are a registered secondary realism check.
No null partition may be selected or discarded after outcomes are observed.

## Plastic-heredity outcomes and ordinary-process baseline

Primary outcome:

- run3 renewal within F8.

Registered process summaries:

- inherited-boundary fraction;
- run5;
- survival;
- mean and minimum parent-daughter cosine;
- renewal time;
- transition count;
- edited-state entropy, occupied types, top-one abundance share, and
  catalytic throughput.

For opposite-half forecasting, use fixed five-fold whole-matrix cross-fitting
separately by candidate and direction. The ordinary-process baseline contains:

- the frozen edited-state renewal prediction;
- source-half renewal frequency;
- source-half inherited-boundary fraction;
- source-half run5 frequency;
- source-half survival;
- source-half mean cosine;
- whole-system mutual information;
- active dimensions and transition count.

A fixed `C=1` ridge-logistic model is fitted on development folds only. The
full model adds only `T_temporal`. Standardization and missing-value medians
are learned on training matrices. Branch-level Bernoulli log loss is evaluated
on the independent target half; inference remains matrix-blocked. No penalty,
feature, nonlinear transform, or fold may be selected from PX9 outcomes.

## Registered primary families and gates

Use 4,096 whole-matrix bootstrap draws, 4,096 paired whole-matrix sign
randomizations, and Holm correction within each named family. Candidates and
source/target halves remain separate.

### 1. Plastic-heredity manipulation validity

All four candidate-by-target-half cells must show:

- positive `Q100 - Q00` renewal probability;
- positive 95% bootstrap lower bound;
- Holm-adjusted one-sided randomization `p < 0.05`; and
- `RANDOM - NOOP` TOST equivalence within +/-0.025.

The within-state Spearman association between the six registered edit
quantiles and independently realized renewal must also be positive with a
positive lower bound and adjusted `p < 0.05` in all four cells.

### 2. Temporal authenticity

At primary support, require all of the following:

- positive `Q100 - Q00` response of `T_temporal` in both candidates and both
  source halves;
- positive A-versus-B reliability separately in both candidates;
- positive source-to-opposite-half renewal forecast in all four directions;
- positive within-state dose concordance between `T_temporal` and
  opposite-half renewal in all four directions.

Every cell requires a positive point estimate, positive 95% lower bound, and
Holm-adjusted `p < 0.05` within its family.

### 3. Beta-topology specificity

Apply the same causal-response and opposite-half forecast tests to
`T_topology`. A beta-topology-specific result requires every response and
forecast cell to pass. Cross-matrix beta controls are reported but cannot
replace the registered size-matched comparison.

### 4. Behavioral nonredundancy

Adding `T_temporal` to the fixed ordinary-process baseline must yield positive
cross-fitted log-loss gain with a positive 95% lower bound and Holm-adjusted
`p < 0.05` in both candidates and both forecast directions.

## Registered classifications

Classify the pilot by the first matching rule:

1. `beta_topology_specific_nonredundant_gauge` if manipulation validity,
   temporal authenticity, beta-topology specificity, and behavioral
   nonredundancy all pass.
2. `generic_transition_information_gauge` if manipulation validity, temporal
   authenticity, and behavioral nonredundancy pass but topology specificity
   fails.
3. `reliable_behavioral_echo` if manipulation validity and temporal
   authenticity pass but behavioral nonredundancy fails.
4. `finite_sample_or_marginal_explanation` if manipulation validity passes but
   temporal authenticity fails.
5. `plastic_heredity_manipulation_invalid` if the causal dose experiment fails.

Mixed or incomplete cells remain visible and cannot be pooled to rescue a
classification.

## Registered secondary analyses

- Raw `T_beta` response at 64 and 128 source branches.
- `Q100 - NOOP` and `NOOP - Q00` decomposition.
- `RANDOM - NOOP` information contrasts.
- Cross-matrix-beta partition contrasts.
- Whole MI and within-part MI components.
- Public molecular/self/revised nine-atom Phi-r at maximum support as a
  non-winning negative control.
- Renewal-time, run5, survival, entropy, concentration, and throughput
  associations.
- A deterministic concordant-outcome diagnostic restricted to common-stream
  branches with the same renewal and survival class in Q00 and Q100. This is
  explicitly post-treatment conditioning and descriptive, never a causal
  mediation analysis.

No secondary result can rescue a failed primary family.

## Validation, execution, and integrity

Before registration, tests must cover at least:

- legal edit enumeration and deterministic empirical quantiles;
- uniform random-edit selection stream separation;
- arm-free paired future streams;
- exact no-op simulator parity;
- strict inheritance and run3 horizon edges;
- explicit transition boundaries;
- temporal derangements preserving marginals and removing self-pairs;
- random partitions being deterministic, exhaustive, disjoint, and
  size-matched;
- equality of the new target scorer with the frozen PX8 scorer;
- simultaneous label-permutation invariance on a fixed dropped coordinate;
- whole-matrix bootstrap, randomization, folds, and replay;
- frozen-student serialization and source hashes.

The scientific pilot runs detached, checkpointed per matrix, with at most
eight workers and at most 30 process CPU-hours. The work directory may be on a
larger local volume; its exact path is operational and not scientific. Compact
reports remain trackable. Dense tables, checkpoints, logs, arrays, and replay
artifacts remain ignored by Git.

## Claim boundaries

PX9 can determine whether the PX8 extension is best described as:

- a beta-topology-specific information gauge;
- a generic transition-information gauge;
- a reliable but behaviorally redundant echo of plastic heredity; or
- a residual finite-sample/marginal artifact.

It cannot make the information statistic causal, rescue the public nine-atom
Phi-r, establish a universal Phi-r/plastic-heredity law, or support claims of
consciousness, agency, life, biological memory, real prebiotic chemistry,
origin-of-life universality, Platonic space, or the Ruliad.
