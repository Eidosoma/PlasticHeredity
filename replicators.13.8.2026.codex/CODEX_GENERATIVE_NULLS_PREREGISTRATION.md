# GN1 preregistration: catalytic-versus-geometric generative null decomposition

## Status and purpose

GN1 is a new reviewer-response program. It is separate from the completed
strict-eight prediction program and from the closed CR1--CR10 intervention
program. No prior result is replaced, rescued, or reinterpreted by GN1.

The reviewer question is whether the observed `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3` (F12) process,
its state dependence, strict coherent-eight occurrence, and outgoing-rule
steerability exceed what is generated by mass-40/mass-80 growth, daughter
sampling, continuation, sparsity, and survival geometry alone.

GN1 is a decomposition, not a requirement that every null have zero events.
The scientifically informative possibilities include a nonzero geometric floor
with catalytic amplification, catalytic state dependence, and/or catalytic
steerability.

## Frozen simulator and endpoints

The reference arm uses the already sealed Codex candidate 02 and 03 contracts
without alteration. Inheritance is strict unrounded float64 cosine similarity
`H > 0.9`. `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3` is a break (`H <= 0.9`) followed strictly later,
within F12, by three consecutive inherited fissions. Extinction before
certification is negative; certification before later extinction remains
positive.

The strict endpoint is the already sealed F32 `primary_all8` event: after a
break, eight consecutive inherited fissions whose eight daughters are mutually
strictly more similar than 0.90 and each no more than 0.85 similar to the
pre-break parent. The registered first-five and centroid variants remain
secondary and cannot replace the all-eight endpoint.

## Fresh cohort

- 96 entirely fresh original catalytic matrices;
- one fresh mass-40 initial composition per matrix, shared across candidates;
- both Codex candidates;
- natural or null-specific untreated main trajectories;
- restored generations 20, 35, 50, 65, and 80;
- no state selection by risk, history, survival, or future outcome;
- the existing 100-attempt main-path retry contract, with no matrix replacement;
- no retry of any scientific future.

The catalytic matrix is always the inference unit. Candidates, mechanisms,
landmarks, branches, and intervention arms from one matrix remain together in
all resampling.

## Four frozen mechanisms

### NATURAL_GARD

The original heterogeneous beta generates the main trajectory and every future.
This is the reference reconstructed GARD process.

### HOMOGENEOUS_GENERATIVE

Every beta entry is the fixed theoretical arithmetic mean of the registered
lognormal prior,

`exp(A + sigma^2 / 2) = exp(4)` for `(A, sigma) = (-4, 4)`.

The same constant is used for every matrix, candidate, landmark, and future.
The homogeneous matrix generates its own main trajectory. It retains the exact
Poisson growth, leaving, overshoot, fission, selected-daughter, mass, and
survival contracts while removing molecule-specific catalytic information.

### COUPLING_DERANGED

The natural heterogeneous beta first generates the natural main trajectory.
Only after a natural landmark is restored, a matrix-level fixed-point-free
permutation is applied simultaneously to beta rows and columns while the
composition and all observed history remain unchanged. The same permutation is
used at all landmarks and in both candidates for that matrix.

This operation exactly preserves every beta entry, the weight distribution,
row/column structure up to relabeling, singular values, eigenvalues, asymmetry,
and global norms, but severs the evolved beta-to-composition label alignment.
It is deliberately not applied before lineage generation, because an IID beta
prior is label-exchangeable and such a pre-lineage shuffle would be a
distributionally identical pseudo-null.

### FISSION_ONLY_GENERATIVE

This explicit non-catalytic process generates its own main trajectory. At each
generation it fills the current daughter to mass 80 by IID uniform molecule
arrivals, performs the candidate-specific registered partition, and continues
the candidate-specific selected daughter. It has no beta-dependent joining,
leaving, or state-dependent kinetics. Its recorded growth count is the exact
number of uniform arrivals. It therefore retains composition, mass, partition,
daughter selection, continuation, and cosine geometry while removing catalytic
state dependence and kinetic turnover.

For frozen-predictor scoring only, the absent fission-only beta input is encoded
by the same positive homogeneous constant matrix. This is a declared
out-of-distribution transfer diagnostic, not a claim that fission-only dynamics
contain catalysis.

## Futures and common random streams

For every restored state and mechanism:

- launch 64 untreated F32 futures;
- use their first 12 fissions for F12 outcomes;
- fix branch halves A = 0--31 and B = 32--63;
- compute strict F32 endpoints from the complete records;
- launch 32 F12 futures for each of `SOURCE_RULE_UP`, `SOURCE_RULE_DOWN`, and
  `RANDOM`;
- reuse branches 0--31 of the untreated F32 futures as the intervention `NOOP`.

The source-rule edits are selected from the matrix's original heterogeneous
beta and the mechanism-specific launch composition using the already frozen
outgoing quantity `x @ beta == beta.T @ x`. This transports the same causal
rule into each generative mechanism and asks whether its effect survives after
the original catalytic coupling is removed. `RANDOM` is uniform over all legal
one-molecule substitutions from a separate selection stream.

Future seed keys omit mechanism and intervention arm, producing common random
streams rather than identical realized futures after dynamics diverge. Matrix,
initial-state, main-path, coupling permutation, random-edit, future, bootstrap,
randomization, smoke, and replay streams are domain-separated.

## Frozen prediction comparisons

### Archived algorithm transfer

The immutable 5x candidate-separated F12 archive with SHA-256
`9b3305a7fed11f432651926d34903443e9413ed299c5d0f1056a0b5fde9990af`
is scored without refitting. GN1 reports branch-level log-loss gain of its
registered `FULL_STATE_GRAPH_HISTORY` prediction over its registered
`DIRECT_HISTORY_PHASE` prediction in each candidate, mechanism, and branch
half. It also reports a five-standard-deviation feature-envelope warning rate.
This is transfer of the manuscript algorithm, not null-specific training.

### Null-specific clean predictor

GN1 additionally performs a frozen two-way whole-matrix cross-fit within each
candidate and mechanism. The baseline is the unique H10 direct block. The only
added block contains composition/state features with no beta-derived
interaction. The direct baseline is unpenalized; the added state block is
standardized using training matrices only and fitted as an offset-ridge
correction with fixed penalties 0.1 for candidate 02 and 0.01 for candidate 03,
copied from the sealed corrected natural-development model. No null-outcome
hyperparameter search is allowed.

This comparison asks whether fission geometry itself creates learnable
composition dependence. It does not isolate beta-conditioned interactions.

## Registered outcomes

For every candidate and mechanism report:

1. F12 `JOINT_BREAK_RUN3`, break, inherited-boundary, and survival prevalence.
2. Statewise branch-half reliability, both overall and after centering within
   catalytic matrix, with whole-matrix confidence intervals.
3. Frozen full-versus-direct log-loss and ordinary branch-level Brier gains in
   halves A and B.
4. Cross-fitted state-plus-H10 versus H10 gains in halves A and B.
5. F32 break, any post-break run8, strict all-eight, first-five, centroid, and
   survival prevalence.
6. `SOURCE_RULE_UP - SOURCE_RULE_DOWN`, each targeted arm versus NOOP, and
   `RANDOM - NOOP` F12 effects.
7. Natural-minus-null differences and natural-minus-null rule-effect
   attenuation for each of the three nulls.
8. Growth-update, occupied-type, entropy, mass, and extinction diagnostics.

## Inference

- 4,096 whole-matrix bootstrap draws for every major interval;
- 4,096 whole-matrix paired sign randomizations for directional contrasts;
- Holm adjustment within each registered six-cell natural-versus-null family
  and within each eight-cell candidate-by-mechanism intervention family;
- 95% intervals for directional effects;
- 90% intervals for equivalence tests;
- `+/-0.025` F12-probability TOST margin for `RANDOM - NOOP` and for null
  outgoing-rule effects;
- no statement of equivalence merely because an interval crosses zero;
- candidates are never pooled to rescue disagreement.

There is no single omnibus pass gate. GN1 reports a preregistered mechanistic
decomposition:

- **geometric floor**: event prevalence remaining under homogeneous and
  fission-only generation;
- **catalytic frequency contribution**: natural-minus-null prevalence;
- **catalytic state-dependence contribution**: reliability and prediction-gain
  differences;
- **catalytic strict-episode contribution**: natural-minus-null strict-event
  occurrence;
- **catalytic steerability contribution**: natural outgoing-rule separation
  and its attenuation or equivalence under nulls.

## Integrity, validation, and stopping

Before one scientific matrix is generated, freeze and hash this document,
source, cohort sizes, mechanisms, constants, endpoints, seeds, feature/model
contracts, resampling, equivalence margins, and report schema. Mandatory tests
cover homogeneous invariance, fixed-point-free coupling derangement, exact beta
multiset and spectral preservation, post-restoration shuffling, fission-only
mass/partition semantics, endpoint edges, legal edits, seed separation, common
future streams, archived predictions, cross-fit matrix separation, matrix-block
resampling, checkpoint integrity, no-op equivalence to the ordinary simulator,
and exact replay.

A smoke test may exercise I/O and integrity only and may not disclose rates,
effect directions, arm ordering, or candidate differences. Every scientific
future receives a complete deterministic replay. Failed/extinct matrices and
futures are never replaced. GN1 stops after its result is checksum-sealed; no
new null or intervention family may be added after outcomes are seen.

## Claim boundaries

GN1 may quantify which portions of F12 and strict-event behavior arise from
sampling/fission geometry and which require catalytic context. It may show that
catalysis modulates frequency, reproducible state dependence, strict episodes,
or steerability.

GN1 cannot establish life, biological memory, agency, autonomous organization,
an installed compotype, real prebiotic chemistry, Phi/PhiID, or a universal
origin-of-life mechanism. Cross-code agreement remains agreement within the
reconstructed GARD family. A nonzero geometric floor must be reported at full
prominence and must not be renamed catalytic heredity.
