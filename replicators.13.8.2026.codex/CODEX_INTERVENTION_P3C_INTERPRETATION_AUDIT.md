# P3c sealed-data interpretation audit

This audit is additive to the checksum-sealed P3c pilot and confirmation. It
generates no catalytic matrices, states, interventions, or simulated futures.
It cannot change either registered P3c gate.

The audit will:

- attribute the registered confirmation failure to its individual gates;
- compare the pilot and confirmation without pooling candidates;
- reconstruct every intervention beta matrix from the sealed base matrices and
  edge tables;
- measure catalytic throughput, incoming and outgoing support dispersion,
  occupied-block norm and sum, Perron radius, leading singular value,
  asymmetry, reciprocity, and concentration;
- describe unconditional break and renewal timing, growth, diversity, and
  matrix influence;
- relate pre-future geometry changes to state-level outcome changes using the
  catalytic matrix as the bootstrap unit; and
- show sensitivity to equivalence margins without changing the registered
  `+/-0.025` decision.

Any geometry/outcome relationship produced here is exploratory association,
not a mediation estimate. In particular, the audit will not condition on a
break caused by an intervention. Causal post-break recovery requires a fresh
shared-natural-break experiment.
