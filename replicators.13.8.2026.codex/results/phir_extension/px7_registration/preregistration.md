## Separation and question

PX7 is additive to the completed Chapter 5 and PX1--PX6 results. Those results
remain unchanged: plastic heredity was controllable, the exact public revised
Phi-r did not behave as a common scalar gauge in Codex, and the high-dimensional
full-block statistic was support-sensitive. PX7 is the final bounded rescue
attempt. It asks whether a preregistered member of a finite Phi-r family can
prospectively gauge either hereditary resistance or post-break resilience.

Strict-eight is excluded. No Fable code, matrices, states, seeds, models, or
outcomes enter PX7. Fable comparisons are descriptive and never a Codex gate.

## Fresh cohort and causal arms

- 24 entirely fresh catalytic matrices, shared by candidates 02 and 03.
- Natural landmarks 20, 35, 50, 65, and 80.
- Frozen CR5 break and renewal students, copied without refit or recalibration.
- 64 common-random-stream futures per arm; A is branches 0--31 and B is 32--63.
- One legal mass-preserving molecular substitution, with observed history held
  fixed at the instantaneous edit.
- Complete deterministic replay. No matrix, path, break, extinction, or adverse
  result is replaced.

Resistance begins from every natural landmark. Arms are BREAK_DOWN (predicted
stabilization), BREAK_UP (predicted destabilization), RANDOM, and NOOP. The
primary endpoint is survival through F6 with no strict inheritance break.

Resilience independently follows each restored landmark for at most 60 natural
fissions to its first strict break. Every arm starts from the identical saved
post-break daughter. Arms are RENEWAL_UP, RENEWAL_DOWN, RANDOM, and NOOP. The
primary endpoint is run3 within F8; certification before later extinction stays
positive. At least 20 distinct eligible matrices per candidate are required.
Failure to meet that threshold stops the resilience decision without replacing
sources.

## Frozen information family

Future information is estimated from explicit transitions within branches;
branch boundaries are never joined. The shared instantaneous edit transition is
not duplicated into each branch. Molecular clocks retain growth and fission
transitions. Generational clocks retain parent-to-selected-daughter transitions.

The Cartesian family is frozen as:

- clocks: molecular and generational;
- partitions: self (source-half lagged-MI Fiedler), past (Fiedler split from the
  preceding 512 natural observations), and beta (Fiedler split of symmetrized
  log1p beta);
- functionals: the public revised nine-atom Phi-r sum, unnormalized
  whole-minus-parts, and its whole-MI-normalized ratio.

This gives 18 eligible formulations. CLR/drop-last, active-coordinate
standardization, atom orientation, the PhiID lattice, covariance regularization,
and deterministic tie rules are frozen in source. All 16 atoms and support
diagnostics are retained. Atom subsets and alternative weights cannot pass.

For A-to-B, Phi-r is estimated only from A and predicts outcomes only in B; the
reverse direction uses B to predict A. This prevents stochastic reuse between
the reading and its tested outcome. Full-dimensional typeset and ratio members
must additionally retain their causal-response sign at a 16-branch source
prefix and be finite for at least 90% of registered readings.

## Inference and gates

The catalytic matrix is the inference unit. All states, arms, halves, acquired
breaks, and repeated landmarks from a matrix remain together. Use 4,096
whole-matrix bootstrap draws and 4,096 purpose-keyed whole-matrix sign draws.
Outcome validity uses Holm correction. Formulation reliability, forecast, and
causal-response families use a studentized max-T correction across all eligible
formulations, axes, candidates, and cross-half directions. Candidates cannot be
pooled to rescue disagreement.

An axis manipulation is valid only when its stabilizing-minus-destabilizing
outcome is positive, its 95% whole-matrix bootstrap lower bound is positive,
its adjusted randomization p is below 0.05 in both candidates and both target
halves, and RANDOM is equivalent to NOOP by a +/-0.025 TOST margin.

A formulation passes an eligible axis only when:

1. source-half readings are positively reliable between A and B;
2. higher source-half Phi-r predicts higher opposite-half resistance or renewal;
3. the stabilizing edit raises Phi-r relative to the destabilizing edit;
4. every effect is positive with positive 95% lower bound and family-wise
   adjusted p below 0.05 in every registered cell;
5. its support rule, manipulation validity, complete replay, and readback pass.

Any passing formulation on either axis yields only
`axis_specific_phi_r_gauge_supported`. The exact self-partitioned molecular
revised member is separately labeled a public-formulation result. A fixed
partition is a named extension, not a rescue of the exact published scorer. A
two-axis gauge requires the same formulation to pass resistance and resilience.
Different winning formulations cannot be combined after outcomes.

Fixed five-fold whole-matrix cross-fitting reports whether each reading adds
branch-level log-loss information beyond a calibrated frozen CR5 student. It is
secondary and cannot rescue a failed primary gate.

## Randomness, execution, and artifacts

Matrix, initial state, main path, acquisition, edit selection, random edit,
future, bootstrap, randomization, replay, smoke, and validation streams are
domain-separated. Arm identity is absent from future seeds. No-op parity with
the plain simulator is mandatory.

The scientific run is detached, checkpointed after each matrix, limited to
eight workers and 30 process CPU-hours, and requires at least 1.5 GB free at
launch. A non-scientific benchmark must project completion within the ceiling.
Only compact state, edit, branch-outcome, information, inference, and replay
artifacts are retained; raw molecular trajectories are not persisted. Generated
checkpoints, compressed tables, arrays, and logs remain ignored by Git.

## Claim boundary

A pass supports only a formulation-specific simulated information-statistical
gauge of resistance and/or resilience in the named Codex GARD contracts. It does
not make Phi-r causal and does not establish consciousness, agency, life,
biological memory, real prebiotic chemistry, a universal origin-of-life
mechanism, or a literal Platonic-space or Ruliad interpretation.
