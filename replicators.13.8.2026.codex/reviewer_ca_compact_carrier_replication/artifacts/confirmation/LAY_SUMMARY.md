# Lay summary

This test asks whether a tiny inherited signal can repeatedly steer a fresh cellular automaton lineage toward one of two ancestral patterns.

It worked: the full carrier replicated under ordinary conditions, and the 64-bit Walsh carrier passed every registered persistence, break, rescue, reversal, and moderate-damage test. This supports a compact causal carrier inside this CA system.

It does not show transfer to a different substrate: all evidence here remains inside the same cellular-automaton substrate.
