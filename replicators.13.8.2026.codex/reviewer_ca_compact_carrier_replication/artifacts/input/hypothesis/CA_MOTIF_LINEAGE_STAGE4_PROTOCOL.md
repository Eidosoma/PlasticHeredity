# Preregistered CA motif-lineage Stage 4: compression and robustness

## Purpose and ancestry

Stage 3R confirmed `simple--strict-49-64--gain-050` on 96 sealed matched
founder pairs. Stage 4 freezes that writer window, daughter-side gain, reader,
visible reset, rule, scores, and causal thresholds. It asks how small the
inherited carrier can become, how much copying damage it tolerates, and whether
the resulting mechanism remains specific to ancestral history.

This is a synthetic CA experiment. A positive result is not evidence of
metabolism, agency, biological life, nonphysical memory, or the full Ruliad.
No Wagner or Fable implementation source may be read, imported, hashed, or
executed.

## Frozen cohorts and review gate

All Stage 1, 2, 3, and 3R scientific pair identities are exposed. Of the 382
unused rule-31649 pairs, a semantic hash ordering assigns 96 to Stage-4
selection and the next 128 to confirmation. The remaining 158 are reserved
untouched for Stage 5. The rule-31648 and rule-70366 banks are already exposed
by earlier CA work and therefore form an exploratory transfer panel only.

The default invocation may fit, screen, qualify, stress, transfer, and
adjudicate, but it cannot simulate the 128 confirmation trajectories. It must
stop at a human-review gate. Confirmation is a separate `confirm` invocation
requiring both `--resume` and `--authorize-confirmation`. No candidate,
threshold, codec, or stress setting may change after selection.

## Frozen lineage mechanism

Every daughter starts from the same bitwise-identical visible board. It reads
the decoded inherited carrier during sweeps 1--32, evolves for 64 sweeps under
process noise 0.002, and writes a raw 512-entry 3x3 motif-energy carrier from
sweeps 49--64. The daughter applies the frozen gain 0.50 and encodes the result
for the next boundary. The founder carrier is encoded before generation 1 but
is not gain-corrected. Checkpoints are generations 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16.

Only the latent payload crosses a boundary. A universal decoder reconstructs
the 512 reader values. Codec bases and quantizer scales are fixed shared
machinery, not lineage-specific payload; both inherited payload bits and
shared codebook bits are reported. All transforms are linear through the
origin, all quantizers preserve exact zero, and runtime code receives no
parent, label, target, or desired-form information.

## Codec atlas

The fitting source is only the exposed Stage-3R diagnostic trace slice for
`strict-49-64`. Bases are fitted without lineage labels. The registered atlas
contains:

- identity at float32 and fixed-scale signed 8-, 4-, and ternary 2-bit
  precision;
- uncentred PCA at ranks 256, 128, 64, 32, 16, and 8, at float32, 8, and 4
  bits;
- Walsh-Hadamard coefficients selected by exposed-trace energy at the same
  ranks, at 8 and 4 bits;
- exposed-energy-selected raw motif addresses at the same ranks, at 8 bits;
- one deterministic random orthoprojector ladder at the same ranks, at 8 bits;
- dihedral D4 motif-orbit pooling (102 values) and centre-plus-neighbour-count
  pooling (18 values), at 8 and 4 bits.

Quantizer scales are the per-coordinate maximum absolute coefficient on the
fit traces. Fresh clipping is reported. The identity-float32 codec must be
bitwise equal to an uncompressed Stage-3R trajectory under the Stage-4 RNG
namespace.

## Selection and qualification

The reference screen uses 96 fresh pairs, 16 futures per history, and eight
generations. A codec is screen-eligible only if generation-8 fixed-form
crossover is at least 0.15 with a positive interval lower bound, survival is
at least 0.90, both A and B directions are positive, at least half of pairs are
positive, and the effect retains at least 60 percent of the float32 identity
anchor. The anchor plus the smallest-bit and strongest-effect representative
of each codec family advance, with deterministic tie breaking.

Qualification reuses the selection cohort with 32 futures per history and 16
generations. It applies the full Stage-3R ladder: zero every boundary, decoded
address shuffle, latent-coordinate shuffle, read disabled, founder write
disabled, no rewrite, ablation entering generation 3, correct and opposite
contemporaneous rescue entering generation 4, opposite founder, and one
percent latent sign corruption. Only codecs passing every registered strict
renewal gate qualify.

At most three compressed finalists are frozen: the smallest payload retaining
at least 70 percent of the identity generation-16 effect, the smallest
interpretable codec, and the best worst-case robustness codec at no more than
128 values and 8 bits. Ties prefer fewer payload bits, stronger worst-case
effect, interpretable machinery, and then lexical candidate ID. The float32
identity anchor is always retained separately if it qualifies.

## Robustness and transfer

Qualified candidates receive intact-lineage dose curves for latent erasure
1/5/10/25/50 percent, latent sign corruption 1/5/10/20 percent, process noise
0.002/0.004/0.008/0.016, repair gain 0.35/0.425/0.50/0.575/0.65, and read
duration 16/24/32/40. The moderate joint stress is 10 percent erasure, 5
percent sign corruption, and process noise 0.004. The harsh joint stress is 25
percent erasure, 10 percent sign corruption, and process noise 0.008.

To keep this atlas inside the registered eight-hour wall budget, robustness
uses the first 32 hash-ordered selection pairs, 16 futures per history, and 16
generations. The transfer panel uses all 64 available pairs per secondary
rule, eight futures per history, and 16 generations. These are exploratory
effect maps; neither can alter the sealed confirmation cohort or relax its
gate.

The exploratory transfer panel applies the frozen rule-31649 codec and
reference to all 64 existing rule-31648 and 64 rule-70366 pairs, changing only
the CA update rule. It cannot nominate or rescue a confirmation candidate.

## Confirmation and verdicts

Confirmation uses 128 sealed pairs, 64 futures per history, and 16
generations. The identity anchor and up to three frozen finalists receive the
complete causal ladder under ordinary conditions and moderate joint stress.
Pair-cluster bootstrap intervals use 10,000 resamples and alpha 0.005 per
confirmed codec.

Verdicts are hierarchical:

- `ROBUST_COMPACT_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: at most 64 values and 8 bits
  passes the strict ladder ordinarily and under moderate joint stress;
- `COMPACT_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: that compact payload passes only the
  ordinary strict ladder;
- `COMPRESSED_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: at most 128 values and 8 bits
  passes ordinarily;
- `HIGH_DIMENSIONAL_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`: only a larger compressed
  payload passes;
- `FULL_CARRIER_ONLY`: only the float32 identity anchor passes;
- `NO_FRESH_STAGE3R_REPLICATION`: the identity anchor fails.

The full Pareto frontier remains part of the result. Stage 5 never launches
automatically. It may be planned after review if the fresh identity anchor
replicates, using the Stage-4 frontier to determine the payload to localize.

## Execution and artifacts

Reference invocations use at most 20 workers and eight wall hours. New
scientific submissions stop with 30 minutes reserved. Checkpoints and all
design/model/cohort/protocol hashes are atomic and resume-bound. The campaign
writes codec models and audit, cohort ledger, screen, qualification,
robustness, transfer, selection decision, confirmation design, pollable
status/log/PID files, reports, queue, and final results. Incomplete work cannot
pass a scientific gate.
