# Draft response to the reviewer

We thank the reviewer for asking whether the reported plastic-heredity process
is specific to the compositional substrate studied here.  We treat this as a
new substrate-transfer hypothesis, not as a claim already established by the
present manuscript.

To test it prospectively, we implemented two contrasting published cellular-
automaton contracts: a stochastic spatial hypercycle protocell and the
nine-state Evoloop rule.  We retained only the process topology shared with
the original analysis--an inheritance break followed strictly later by three
renewed inherited parent--offspring transitions--and independently defined
individuals, lineage boundaries, and morphology for each CA.  Each model has
its own threshold, fixed as the higher 95th percentile of size-matched
stranger similarities from a disjoint calibration cohort.  Stranger children
must come from another independently seeded world block.  Neither CA is
pooled with the other.

The attached first-stage results are developmental only.  They determine
whether the fixed confirmation experiment is mechanically and statistically
viable, after which the workflow stops.  They are not evidence for the
reviewer's proposed generalization.  Any confirmatory statement will be made
only after a separately authorized, prospectively seeded confirmation cohort.

Use exactly one of the following after that confirmation:

- If one model passes: "The preregistered process transferred to one of the
  two tested CA contracts.  This supports transfer beyond the original
  substrate, but not universality."
- If both pass: "The preregistered process transferred to both contrasting CA
  contracts, providing stronger evidence of cross-substrate robustness while
  remaining limited to these operational definitions."
- If neither passes: "Neither tested CA contract passed its complete
  preregistered gate, so cross-substrate transfer is not supported under these
  implementations."

In every case, the result does not establish life, biological memory,
Phi/PhiID, real chemistry, or a common GARD mechanism.  The unpublished
hypothesis-generating exercise is neither cited nor counted as evidence and
none of its files, code, parameters, seeds, examples, or numerical outcomes
enter this campaign.
