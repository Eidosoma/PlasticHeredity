# Reporting boundary

Report every result with all of these qualifications:

- This campaign tests a claim that the current preprint does not make.
- It is exploratory and reviewer-motivated, not confirmatory preprint evidence.
- It is a clean-room GARD analogue inspired by documents and result data from
  the NewIdeas Wagner GRN work; it is not a Wagner-code replication.
- Candidates 02 and 03 are alternative simulator contracts, not independent
  scientific replications.
- Exact replay establishes computational reproducibility only.
- A successful 100-coordinate carrier is an engineered external side channel,
  not endogenous GARD heredity. A compressed, noisy carrier of at most 32
  coordinates is the stronger result tier.
- A null result limits only the registered carrier family, parameter grid,
  frozen rule cohort, and wall-time tier that actually completed.
- An incomplete wall-time tier receives no scientific verdict.

Do not edit the preprint from this directory. Do not merge these outcomes into
the preprint's evidence tables or describe them as replicated tests.
