# Registered plasticity–persistence protocol

## Full surface

Fresh catalytic matrices run under both reconstructed candidates across the 21
fixed catalytic-strength/leave-rate cells. Each F32 lineage separately records
break supply, recovery conditional on a break, joint F12, run-eight,
coherence, distinctness, complete registered strict eight, terminal diversity,
and stable-form clusters.

The primary trade-off requires a negative whole-matrix F12-versus-strict-eight
surface correlation and confirmation of the fixed `(2,2)` versus mean
`(1,0.5)/(4,2)` contrast in both candidates. The F12 contrast is decomposed
exactly into break-supply and conditional-recovery terms; this is descriptive,
not causal mediation.

## Lineage identity

At `(1,0.5)`, `(1,1)`, `(2,2)`, and `(4,2)`, F12 donors are selected by frozen
hash order. B is the third daughter completing the earliest post-break run of
three. A separately labelled strict-eight donor uses the eighth daughter of its
first qualifying window when available.

Fresh F32 futures start from two independent native-B forks, an
abundance-spectrum permutation, and a mass-matched different-lineage B. Target
arrival excludes time zero. Residence, capture, fork similarity, different-
parent similarity, and an attractor census distinguish lineage identity,
shared destination, transient churn, and unresolved cases.

## Contract factorial

All 16 combinations of exposure, overshoot, fission, and retained-daughter
rules run across the complete 21-cell surface on a smaller fresh cohort. The
two candidate corners must reproduce candidates 02 and 03. Exact four-factor
Shapley decompositions attribute candidate differences in F12, strict eight,
terminal similarity, and coupled-twin damage growth. A dominant source requires
an absolute share above 50%, lower confidence bound above 35%, and matching
direction in matrix halves.

## Runtime and integrity

A production-safe benchmark chooses the largest registered A/B/C tier projected
to finish generation, analysis, full replay, and reporting within 6.75 hours.
The campaign soft-stops at 7.5 cumulative hours and is hard-stopped at eight;
restarts cannot reset the ledger. Every stochastic checkpoint must replay
exactly for a complete verdict.
