simulators. It is not evidence for the current preprint, an independent GARD
replication, a Wagner replication, biological heredity, or prebiotic realism.

Earlier GARD and NewIdeas outcomes supplied hypotheses only. They are not
pooled with the fresh cohort, and no Wagner source code, tests, notebooks, or
configuration are read, imported, copied, or executed.
