# Read this before reporting these outputs

The files in this directory are sealed analysis outputs from **one
exploratory, reviewer-motivated follow-up**.  The follow-up tests stronger
lineage-identity, shared-destination, rehoming, and stable-two-form
interpretations that are **not claims made by the current preprint**.

Candidates 02 and 03 are two reconstructed simulator contracts within this
single campaign.  Their agreement is a robustness check, not independent
replication.  Likewise, exact replay tests computational reproducibility; it
is not a scientific replication.

The preprint's central F12 break-and-renewal endpoint is weaker than strict
coherent-eight and is not tested by this follow-up.  A `FAIL` label in
`SCIENTIFIC_REPORT.md` therefore means that an exploratory follow-up gate did
not pass.  It does not mean that a preprint claim failed or that plastic
heredity in general was refuted.

The results are conditional on the selected 50-rule strict-capable cohort and
previously observed strict-B donors.  Rules 11, 54, and 63 are post-hoc case
studies.  All conclusions remain provisional until full exact replay passes.
`NewIdeas` supplied hypotheses only and supplied no evidence.

The complete reporting requirements and safe wording are in
`../../REPORTING_BOUNDARY.md`.
