# Evidence boundary

The retained CA artifacts are used only to formulate and operationalize a
hypothesis. They are not counted as evidence in this replication.

The non-evidential parity layer may establish that the independent engine
decodes states, evolves parents, constructs local observers, pools calibration
histories, and writes carriers consistently with retained data. Because raw
daughter trajectories and a complete semantic-randomness specification were
not retained, it explicitly does not claim exact reader-trajectory parity.

Fresh evidence begins only with daughter checkpoints created after the local
Stage-1 registration is sealed. Eligible parents come from the frozen donor
atlas, but every donor named by any retained round-3, Stage-1, Stage-2,
development, or parity pair is excluded. Remaining donors are paired without
outcomes, under a density caliper and deterministic hash tie breaks, with no
reuse.

A positive Stage 1 means local motif memories causally control one generation
under the registered interventions. A positive Stage 2 means the same fixed
reader transfers across the already-registered pairs, reset environments,
transformations, controls, noise, doses, and densities. Neither result shows
that daughters rewrite the carrier, transmit it for multiple generations, or
meet the paper's full definition of Plastic Heredity.
